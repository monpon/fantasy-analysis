# The best team in the league by all-play is 0-2

Through Week 2

*master(rage)baiters have the top all-play record, the best lineup management, and no wins.*

master(rage)baiters are 0-2, and it's hard to find a team that deserves it less. They rank 3rd in points per game (147.1) and 1st in all-play (15-3). They lost Week 1 to the week's highest score (174.3) and Week 2 by 2.4 to the week's 2nd-highest score. Their record is 1.7 wins below what their scores predict, the biggest gap in the league.

### What's working

Nearly everything. Josh Allen has averaged 38.2 per start, the highest of any player with two starts in the league. Jahmyr Gibbs has 56.9 over two starts. The team leads the league at both quarterback and running back, and its lineup efficiency, 94.5%, is the best in the league. Their bench has scored just 82.4 points, the fewest of any team.

### What isn't

The receivers rank 7th (63.5). Tetairoa McMillan (25.6 over two starts), Rashee Rice (22.2) and Luther Burden III (15.7) have started both weeks. Kicker ranks 9th (Harrison Mevis, 5.0), and George Kittle's 22.2 puts tight end 7th. Bo Nix has scored 19.6 on the bench behind Allen.

### Outlook

The remaining schedule is 6th-hardest. Teams that play like this usually start winning. The biggest risk is the hole: at 0-2, they have little margin for more bad luck. Nix is a tradeable asset for receiver help.
