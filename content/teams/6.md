# gibbs and friends are still better than their record

Through Week 5

*The renamed team got back on track against GDID, but 2-3 is still 1.2 wins short of what its scores predict.*

gibbs and friends (formerly master(rage)baiters) are 2-3 and 5th in the standings. They rank 4th in points per game (142.9) and 2nd in all-play (29-16), and the record is 1.2 wins below what the scores predict. They beat GDID 124.3 to 90.5 in Week 5.

### What's working

The team's namesake, Jahmyr Gibbs, has 30.7 per start, the best of any player with five starts. Josh Allen has 28.6 per start. They rank 1st at quarterback and 3rd at running back. Lineup efficiency (90.5%) is the best in the league.

### What isn't

Receiver ranks 8th: Luther Burden III has 11.4 per start and Rashee Rice 9.5. Kicker ranks last. Omarion Hampton (9.9 per start) and David Montgomery (9.1, now listed out) have been ordinary next to Gibbs.

### Outlook

The 6th-hardest remaining schedule is fair. Week 6 is a problem: Gibbs is on bye with Detroit, and the current lineup projects to 121.4, 19.0 behind first-place Net Worth: 1.4b.
