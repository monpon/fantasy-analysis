# The league's best team finally has a win

Through Week 3

*master(rage)baiters lead the league in points and all-play, and needed a Monday night comeback to get to 1-2.*

master(rage)baiters are 1-2 and 6th in the standings, and it's hard to find a team that deserves that less. They lead the league in points per game (152.0) and all-play (22-5), and have scored more total points (455.9) than anyone. They've scored between 143.6 and 161.8 every week, the most consistent scoring in the league. The record is 1.4 wins below what the scores predict, the biggest gap in the league.

The first win came the hard way: down 154.2 to 142.0 entering Monday night, with Luther Burden III left, they needed 12.3 and got 19.8.

### What's working

Jahmyr Gibbs (32.8 per start) and Josh Allen (31.3) are the best RB and QB in the league by production. The team ranks 1st at quarterback and 2nd at running back. Lineup efficiency (91.3%) is the best in the league, and only 43.6 points have been left on the bench across three weeks, the fewest of any team.

### What isn't

Receiver ranks 7th. Rashee Rice (12.7 per start), Burden (11.8) and Tetairoa McMillan (9.8) are ordinary. Kicker ranks 9th. Bo Nix has scored 43.7 on the bench behind Allen, and Jordan Addison 24.1.

### Outlook

The 7th-hardest remaining schedule gives them a fair chance to climb. Teams that play like this usually win. Nix is valuable trade material for a receiver upgrade.
