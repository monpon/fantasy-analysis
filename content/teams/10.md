# Washington has lost four straight and is running out of healthy players

Through Week 7

*Injuries to Davante Adams and others have thinned a roster that started 2-1.*

The Washington football team is 2-5 and 9th in the standings after four straight losses. It ranks 7th in points per game (130.0) and 7th in all-play (29-34), and the record is 1.2 wins below what the scores predict, the biggest negative gap in the league. In Week 7 it set a perfect lineup and still scored 93.6, the week's lowest, because its bench had almost nothing available.

### What's working

The running backs rank 2nd: Christian McCaffrey has 20.6 per start and Javonte Williams 18.6, and both have started every week. Brock Purdy has 21.1 per start, and QB ranks 2nd. Matthew Golden has 17.3 per start.

### What isn't

Injuries and depth. Davante Adams (17.2 per start), Romeo Doubs and Jacory Croskey-Merritt are on IR. Receiver ranks 9th, kicker 9th and D/ST T-8th. In Weeks 4 and 5, the losses came with winning lineups left on the bench.

### Outlook

Washington faces the 3rd-hardest remaining schedule. Week 8 adds the San Francisco bye, which takes out McCaffrey and Purdy, so newly added Kirk Cousins starts at quarterback.
