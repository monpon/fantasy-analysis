# Washington's Week 4 loss came down to the quarterback choice

Through Week 4

*Washington had 166.2 points available and scored 121.7, losing by 3.3.*

The Washington football team is 2-2 and 4th in the standings. It ranks 4th in points per game (143.1) and 3rd in all-play (22-14), so the record roughly matches the scores. The Week 4 loss to Net Worth: 1.4b, 125.0 to 121.7, was the closest game of the week.

### What's working

The backfield still leads the league (235.6 points). Javonte Williams has 20.4 per start, including 31.3 in Week 4, and Christian McCaffrey 18.5. Tight end now ranks 2nd: Sam LaPorta scored 22.4 in Week 4 and has 14.2 per start. QB ranks 2nd overall.

### What isn't

In Week 4, Washington started newly added Kyler Murray (11.5) while Brock Purdy (19.6) and Jalen Hurts (13.5) scored on the bench. Romeo Doubs (23.8) and Carnell Tate (21.5) also scored on the bench that week. The 44.5 points left behind cost the game.

Receiver ranks 9th. Davante Adams has 18.5 per start but scored 7.2 in Week 4, and Denzel Boston has 56.4 bench points without a start. With three quarterbacks and a deep bench, Washington's bench has scored 339.8 points, 2nd-most in the league.

### Outlook

The 7th-hardest remaining schedule is manageable. Purdy is back in the lineup for Week 5. Settling on one quarterback and finding a consistent WR2 would turn the depth into wins.
