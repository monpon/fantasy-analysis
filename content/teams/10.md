# Washington has lost five straight and is in last place

Through Week 8

*Injuries keep piling up, and the toughest remaining schedule in the league is ahead.*

The Washington football team is 2-6 and last in the standings after five straight losses. It ranks 8th in points per game (127.6) and 8th in all-play (29-43), and the record is 1.2 wins below what the scores predict. In Week 8, Kirk Cousins scored 28.2 at quarterback, but Washington scored 110.9, the week's lowest.

### What's working

Christian McCaffrey (20.6 per start) and Javonte Williams (17.1) have started every week they've been available, and RB ranks 3rd. Brock Purdy has 21.1 per start, and QB ranks 3rd. Matthew Golden has 17.9 per start.

### What isn't

Injuries. Davante Adams, Romeo Doubs and Jacory Croskey-Merritt are on IR, and Jeremiyah Love and Carnell Tate are listed out. Receiver ranks 9th, kicker last and D/ST 9th. Denzel Boston has 6.3 per start since moving into the lineup.

### Outlook

Washington faces the hardest remaining schedule in the league. McCaffrey and Purdy are back for Week 9, and ESPN projects Washington as an 8.9-point favorite over Barcelona.
