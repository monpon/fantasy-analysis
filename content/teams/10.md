# Washington ends its losing streak with the week's top score

Through Week 9

*A 166.5 against Barcelona was Washington's best score of the season.*

The Washington football team is 3-6 and 8th in the standings. It ranks 6th in points per game (131.9) and 6th in all-play (38-43), and the record is 1.2 wins below what the scores predict. ESPN's simulations give it 5% playoff odds.

### What's working

With Brock Purdy and Christian McCaffrey back, the lineup scored 166.5 in Week 9. Purdy has 21.8 per start and McCaffrey 20.8, and quarterback and running back both rank 3rd. Matthew Golden has 19.9 per start, including 32.4 in Week 9, and Javonte Williams has 17.7 and has started every week. Lineup efficiency (87.7%) ranks 3rd.

### What isn't

Injuries and depth. Davante Adams, Romeo Doubs, Jeremiyah Love and Jacory Croskey-Merritt are on IR, and Michael Wilson is listed out. Receiver ranks 8th and kicker last. At tight end, Sam LaPorta has 11.6 per start and Tyler Warren 7.8.

### Outlook

Washington has the 6th-hardest remaining schedule. For Week 10, Wilson is still in the lineup as of Tuesday, and ESPN projects Washington 24.0 behind Tennessee.
