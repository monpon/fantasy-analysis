# Washington has lost two straight with better lineups on the bench

Through Week 5

*The league's best backfield, and 404.9 bench points, the most of any team.*

The Washington football team is 2-3 and 7th in the standings, ranking 6th in points per game (138.2) and 6th in all-play (23-22). It lost Week 4 by 3.3 and Week 5 by 17.1. In both weeks, its best possible lineup would have won.

### What's working

Running back still leads the league (269.3): Javonte Williams and Christian McCaffrey have 19.2 and 18.3 per start. Davante Adams has 19.2 per start, and Matthew Golden 17.7 in three starts. QB and TE both rank 2nd.

### What isn't

Lineup decisions. Washington's bench has scored 404.9 points, the most in the league, and lineup efficiency (84.0%) ranks 9th. Denzel Boston has 70.0 bench points without a start, the most of any player in the league. In Week 5, Tyler Warren scored 21.4 on the bench while Jeremiyah Love (2.2) and Carnell Tate (1.6) started. Receiver ranks 9th and D/ST 9th.

### Outlook

The 8th-hardest remaining schedule is manageable. For Week 6, Boston is finally in the starting lineup (projected 13.7), and Sam LaPorta is on bye with Detroit. Jalen Hurts is listed out.
