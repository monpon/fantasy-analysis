# Washington climbs to first on the strength of its backfield

Through Week 3

*The league's best running backs and a 166.2 in Week 3 have Washington on top of the standings, but receiver and tight end are still weak spots.*

The Washington football team is 2-1 and in 1st place, ahead of four other 2-1 teams on points scored. It ranks 2nd in points per game (150.3) and 2nd in all-play (21-6), so the record reflects how well it has played. Scoring has gone up every week: 131.7, 153.0, 166.2. The Week 3 score was the best in the league.

### What's working

The backfield leads the league in RB production (181.8). Christian McCaffrey (19.3 per start), Javonte Williams (16.8), Jeremiyah Love (14.1) and Travis Etienne Jr. (10.3) have all started every week. Brock Purdy has averaged 29.9 over two starts, and the quarterback call has been right all three weeks: whichever of Purdy and Jalen Hurts started has outscored the one on the bench. QB production ranks 2nd, and lineup efficiency (89.1%) ranks 2nd.

### What isn't

Receiver ranks 6th (108.2). Davante Adams (22.3 per start) is the only receiver who has started every week, and the second spot has rotated through Carnell Tate, Romeo Doubs and Matthew Golden. Golden's 21.0 in Week 3 was the best result yet. Denzel Boston has 42.5 bench points without a start.

Tight end ranks 7th. Sam LaPorta has 11.5 per start. Kenyon Sadiq has 38.6 bench points, including 23.5 in Week 3, and T.J. Hockenson has 23.6. Three tight ends and two starting quarterbacks take up a lot of bench space.

### Outlook

The 8th-hardest remaining schedule is favorable. Etienne is now listed out, which takes away one of the four running backs. That makes it a good time to settle the WR2 spot and to trade from the QB or TE surplus.
