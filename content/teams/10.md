# Washington's backfield is carrying a thin receiving corps

Through Week 2

*Four starting running backs and two good quarterbacks have made Washington a top-3 all-play team, but receiver is a weak spot.*

The Washington football team is 1-1 and ranks 4th in points per game (142.3) and 3rd in all-play (12-6), so its record matches its scores. It lost Week 1 to Tennessee, 142.2 to 131.7, then beat master(rage)baiters by 2.4 in the closest game of the season.

### What's working

The quarterback decisions have been right both weeks. Jalen Hurts outscored Brock Purdy in Week 1 (24.7 to 21.1), and Purdy outscored Hurts in Week 2 (28.5 to 16.2), leaving Washington 2nd in QB production. The backfield ranks 3rd: Christian McCaffrey (36.4 over two starts), Javonte Williams (32.2), Travis Etienne Jr. (21.9) and Jeremiyah Love (20.5) have all started both weeks, using both FLEX spots. Lineup efficiency is 88.6%, 3rd in the league.

### What isn't

Starting four running backs leaves one WR spot beside Davante Adams, and it hasn't worked. Carnell Tate scored 7.8 there in Week 1 and Romeo Doubs 12.6 in Week 2. Denzel Boston outscored both from the bench (13.9 and 20.5) and has 34.4 bench points without a start. Adams himself swung from 5.6 to 40.5. WR production ranks 6th.

The roster also carries three tight ends. Sam LaPorta (27.0 over two starts) has started, while T.J. Hockenson (20.5) and Kenyon Sadiq (15.1) have sat. Etienne is now listed out.

### Outlook

The 8th-hardest remaining schedule helps. With Etienne out, the four-RB approach gets harder to run, which makes this a good time to give Boston a start or trade from the QB and TE surplus for a receiver.
