# Fraudulent Failures keep winning despite the most bench points in the league

Through Week 4

*3-1 and 2nd in all-play, even though the lineup has left more points behind than any other team.*

Fraudulent Failures are 3-1 and 2nd in the standings, ranking 3rd in points per game (143.2) and 2nd in all-play (26-10). They beat Barcelona 137.2 to 127.1 in Week 4.

### What's working

Kenneth Walker III has averaged 27.5 per start, the best of any running back with four starts except Jahmyr Gibbs. Zay Flowers has 22.4 per start, Lamar Jackson 19.8, and Trey McBride gives them the league's top tight end production (70.6). The record matches the scores: no luck involved.

### What isn't

Lineup decisions. Efficiency is 81.4%, last in the league, and 130.9 points have been left on the bench over four weeks, the most of any team. Kyle Monangai has 59.3 bench points without a start, including 28.0 in Week 4. Wan'Dale Robinson has 40.4 on the bench. Ja'Marr Chase has 15.1 per start, with 5.7 in Week 4.

### Outlook

The 8th-hardest remaining schedule is favorable. Week 5 brings complications: Walker is on bye with Kansas City, and Jacoby Brissett is starting at quarterback with Lamar Jackson projected for 0 and listed questionable. Better start/sit decisions are still the biggest available improvement.
