# Fraudulent Failures have the talent, but the bench keeps outscoring the lineup

Through Week 2

*The league's 2nd-best all-play team is only 1-1, and 153.9 bench points explain a lot of it.*

On paper, Fraudulent Failures are one of the best teams in the league. They rank 2nd in points per game (149.4) and 2nd in all-play (14-4), and they opened with the Week 1 high score of 174.3. They're 1-1 because they lost Week 2 to Cleveland, 139.6 to 124.6, and that loss had a clear cause. The team's best possible lineup would have scored 151.7 and won.

### What's working

The foundation is strong. Kenneth Walker III has averaged 29.0 per start, D'Andre Swift 22.6, and Trey McBride makes this the league's best tight end group (42.6 over two starts). Lamar Jackson has added 39.8 over two starts. Ja'Marr Chase bounced back from a 3.2 in Week 1 with 26.5 in Week 2.

### What isn't

Lineup efficiency is 80.4%, 9th in the league. Kyle Monangai has scored 28.2 on the bench without a start, Rashod Bateman 22.8 and Mike Evans 16.9. Dallas Goedert had perhaps the worst timing in the league: 23.7 on the bench in Week 1, then 1.4 when he started in Week 2. DK Metcalf has 14.7 in two starts. At D/ST, the team has used two different units for 10.0 total points, 7th in the league.

Injuries are piling up, too. Goedert, Rico Dowdle and Zach Charbonnet are all listed out.

### Outlook

This team has the easiest remaining schedule in the league and the talent to be the favorite. The fix is better start/sit decisions, particularly at receiver and at FLEX, where Monangai has been sitting.
