# Fraudulent Failures lead the league in all-play and are 3-2

Through Week 5

*They scored 159.9 in Week 5, the week's 3rd-best score, and still lost.*

Fraudulent Failures are 3-2 and 3rd in the standings. They rank 2nd in points per game (146.6) and 1st in all-play (33-12), and the record is about 0.7 wins below what the scores predict. In Week 5 they lost to Tennessee's 195.8 despite a near-perfect lineup that left only 6.0 points on the bench.

### What's working

Trey McBride (18.2 per start) gives them the league's top tight end production (92.2). Kenneth Walker III has 27.5 per start, Zay Flowers 20.4, and Lamar Jackson 19.8. The depth showed in Week 5: Jacoby Brissett (25.3) and Mike Evans (26.3) both delivered. QB ranks 3rd.

### What isn't

The bench, over the season. Lineup efficiency is 84.3% (8th). Kyle Monangai has 59.3 bench points without a start, Wan'Dale Robinson 51.7. DK Metcalf has 6.7 per start. Ja'Marr Chase has 16.6 per start, including a 3.2 and a 5.7.

### Outlook

The 8th-hardest remaining schedule is favorable. Week 6 brings more byes: Chase is off with Cincinnati, and Aaron Rodgers is currently starting at quarterback (14.9) with Lamar Jackson (19.3, questionable) on the bench.
