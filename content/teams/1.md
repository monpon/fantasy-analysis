# Fraudulent Failures are winning with a deep, balanced lineup

Through Week 3

*A top-three all-play team with the league's best tight end and the easiest schedule ahead.*

Fraudulent Failures are 2-1 and 2nd in the standings, and rank 3rd in both points per game (145.2) and all-play (19-8). They opened with the Week 1 high score (174.3), lost Week 2 to Cleveland, and beat GDID 136.8 to 119.9 in Week 3, with seven starters in double figures.

### What's working

The starters who have been in the lineup every week have been reliable: Kenneth Walker III (26.4 per start), Lamar Jackson (20.1), Trey McBride (19.7), D'Andre Swift (18.7) and Ja'Marr Chase (18.2). McBride makes this the league's best tight end group (60.5). Zay Flowers has averaged 20.7 in two starts.

### What isn't

Bench decisions. Lineup efficiency is 83.5% (7th), and the bench has scored 201.8 points. Kyle Monangai (31.3), Wan'Dale Robinson (29.4) and Rashod Bateman (28.5) have all scored without starting. DK Metcalf has 7.3 per start. Dallas Goedert scored 23.7 on the bench in Week 1 and 1.4 in his one start. D/ST ranks 7th, with three different units used.

Goedert, Rico Dowdle and Zach Charbonnet are all listed out.

### Outlook

Fraudulent Failures have the easiest remaining schedule in the league. Week 3 showed the lineup working better (13.5 points left on the bench, their best week). If that holds, they're one of the favorites.
