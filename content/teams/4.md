# GDID has the league's best backfield and its weakest receivers

Through Week 7

*3-4, with the league's lowest points-per-game average and a record 0.7 wins ahead of it.*

GDID is 3-4 and 8th in the standings. It ranks last in points per game (114.8) and 9th in all-play (21-42), so the record is about 0.7 wins better than the scores predict. It won Week 6 with 141.7, then lost Week 7 with 98.8.

### What's working

Running back leads the league (375.5), with Bijan Robinson at 20.8 per start and depth from Bhayshul Tuten, Quinshon Judkins and Chase Brown. Ka'imi Fairbairn gives them the top kicker production.

### What isn't

Receiver ranks last (124.2), and the receivers who have scored haven't been starting: KC Concepcion has 62.9 bench points without a start. Quarterback ranks 9th and tight end 8th. Germie Bernard's 25.7 in Week 6 was a bright spot, but he scored 5.7 in Week 7.

### Outlook

GDID has the 4th-hardest remaining schedule. Trevor Lawrence is back at quarterback for Week 8 (projected 20.8). The receiver problem has lasted all season.
