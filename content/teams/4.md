# GDID's first loss shows the problem: no receivers

Through Week 3

*Bijan Robinson scored 35.3 in Week 3 and GDID still lost, and the league's weakest WR group is a big reason why.*

GDID is 2-1 and 5th in the standings, but ranks 8th in points per game (120.6) and 7th in all-play (12-15). The 2-1 record is about 0.7 wins better than the scores predict. That includes a Week 2 win with 99.2 points, which would have beaten only two teams that week.

### What's working

Bijan Robinson has averaged 25.9 per start, with 35.3 in Week 3. The backfield ranks 3rd (167.3), with Chase Brown, Bhayshul Tuten and Quinshon Judkins providing depth. Tight end ranks 2nd, with Travis Kelce and Dalton Kincaid both contributing. Lineup efficiency (87.5%) ranks 4th.

### What isn't

The receivers rank last in the league (61.3). Ladd McConkey has 12.1 per start. Josh Downs scored 12.7 in his first start, after 20.9 on the bench. A.J. Brown (5.6 in his one start) is on IR, and KC Concepcion, Brian Thomas Jr. and Michael Pittman Jr. have combined for 47.1 points, all on the bench.

At tight end, the benched player outscored the starter in each of the first two weeks. QB (8th), kicker (8th) and D/ST (8th) are also below average.

### Outlook

GDID has the 2nd-hardest remaining schedule, and a team averaging 120.6 will struggle to keep its record. Trading running back depth for a real receiver is the clearest move.
