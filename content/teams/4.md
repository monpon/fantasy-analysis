# GDID is 4-4, the luckiest record in the league

Through Week 8

*The lowest points-per-game average in the league, and a .500 record.*

GDID is 4-4 and 6th in the standings. It ranks last in points per game (116.1) and last in all-play (23-49), and its record is 1.4 wins better than the scores predict, the biggest such gap in the league. It beat Washington 125.2 to 110.9 in Week 8.

### What's working

Running back leads the league (437.0). Bijan Robinson has 19.2 per start, and Bhayshul Tuten has 16.9, with 29.9 in Week 8. Kicker ranks 1st.

### What isn't

Receiver ranks last in the league and D/ST last. KC Concepcion has 62.9 bench points against 6.7 in his one start. Bijan has scored 7.3 and 7.6 in two of the last three weeks. Tight end ranks 7th.

### Outlook

The 5th-hardest remaining schedule is middling. ESPN projects GDID as a slight favorite in Week 9, but over the season this team has scored the fewest points.
