# GDID is 2-0, but the scoreboard says average

Through Week 2

*A deep backfield and some timely luck have covered for the league's weakest receiving corps.*

GDID has as many wins as anyone and ranks 8th in points per game (121.0). Their all-play record is 9-9, exactly average, and the 2-0 start is a full win better than their scores predict. The Week 2 win came with 99.2 points, which would have beaten only two teams that week.

### What's working

The running backs. GDID ranks 2nd in RB production, led by Bijan Robinson (21.2 per start) and supported by Chase Brown (30.0 over two starts), Bhayshul Tuten (24.0) and Quinshon Judkins (16.8). They also rank 3rd at tight end by carrying two productive ones.

### What isn't

GDID ranks last in the league in WR production (38.0). Ladd McConkey (25.7 over two starts) is the only receiver who has started both weeks. A.J. Brown scored 5.6 in his one start and is now on IR. Meanwhile, Josh Downs (20.9), KC Concepcion (16.0) and Brian Thomas Jr. (14.0) have all scored on the bench without a start.

The tight end rotation has gone the wrong way twice. Week 1: Travis Kelce started (10.1) while Dalton Kincaid scored 18.0 on the bench. Week 2: Kincaid started (22.5) while Kelce scored 25.1 on the bench. Trevor Lawrence's 6.2 in Week 2 contributed to the 7th-ranked QB production.

### Outlook

GDID has the 3rd-hardest remaining schedule, and a team scoring 121 points a week won't keep winning at this rate. The obvious move is trading running back depth for a real receiver, and giving Downs a start.
