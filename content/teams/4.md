Through Week 5

*Three straight losses, the lowest score of the season, and the league's lowest scoring average.*

### What isn't

The receivers rank last in the league (79.3). Ladd McConkey has 9.1 per start, Josh Downs 8.3, and newly added Roman Wilson scored 0.0 in Week 5. QB ranks 9th, TE 9th and D/ST last. Chase Brown has 13.2 per start, with 7.0 in Week 5.

### Outlook

GDID faces the 2nd-hardest remaining schedule (tied). Kirk Cousins is starting at quarterback in Week 6. Without a real upgrade at receiver, it's hard to see this team scoring enough to climb.
