# GDID's receivers are still the problem

Through Week 4

*Two straight losses, the lowest score of Week 4, and the league's lowest WR production.*

GDID is 2-2 and 7th in the standings. It ranks 8th in points per game (118.2) and 8th in all-play (12-24), and the record is about 0.7 wins better than the scores predict. It has lost two straight, including Week 4's lowest score in the league (110.8).

### What's working

Bijan Robinson has 26.3 per start and has scored at least 27.7 in three of four weeks. The backfield ranks 2nd overall, with Chase Brown at 14.8 per start. Ka'imi Fairbairn's 16.0 in Week 4 helped make kicker the 3rd-best in the league.

### What isn't

The receivers rank last in the league (71.6). Ladd McConkey has 9.1 per start and scored 0.0 in Week 4, and Josh Downs has 8.6. KC Concepcion (30.3) and Brian Thomas Jr. (25.5) have scored only on the bench. At tight end, Travis Kelce has 9.2 per start. Quarterback ranks 9th and D/ST 9th. In Week 4, Quinshon Judkins scored 21.6 on the bench, and the best possible lineup (143.2) would have won.

### Outlook

GDID has the 3rd-hardest remaining schedule, and a team averaging 118 points will find it hard to keep winning. The fix is the same as it has been: turn running back depth into receiver production.
