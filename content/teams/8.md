# Barcelona's new additions deliver a first win

Through Week 3

*Sam Darnold's 27.7 and a near-perfect lineup gave Barcelona its best week, but the hole is deep.*

Barcelona Backshotters are 1-2 and 9th in the standings, ranking 9th in points per game (112.2) and last in all-play (5-22). Week 3 was their best by far: a 132.7 win over The Bagel Busters, with only 4.3 points left on the bench, the smallest gap of any team that week.

### What's working

Recent additions paid off in Week 3. Sam Darnold scored 27.7 in his first start for the team, and Saquon Barkley added 9.0 in his debut. Ashton Jeanty (18.8 per start) and James Cook III (16.7) give Barcelona the 5th-best backfield. D/ST leads the league (34.0), helped by the Vikings' 19.0 in Week 3.

### What isn't

Tight end is the worst position group in the league (8.4 points). Colston Loveland has 2.8 per start, though his 7.1 in Week 3 was a step up. Mark Andrews has 25.2 bench points without a start. Receiver ranks 8th: Malik Nabers has 7.2 per start, Jaylen Waddle 9.8 and George Pickens 10.3, while Xavier Worthy has 23.3 on the bench.

Lineup efficiency is 81.9% (9th), mostly from the first two weeks. Jayden Daniels is listed out.

### Outlook

Barcelona faces the hardest remaining schedule in the league. Week 3 showed a better version of this team: if the lineup keeps being managed that way and Andrews gets a look at tight end, it can compete week to week.
