# Barcelona has climbed back to 3-4

Through Week 7

*After an 0-2 start, Barcelona has averaged 142.5 points over the last five weeks.*

Barcelona Backshotters are 3-4 and 6th in the standings, ranking 6th in points per game (130.9) and 6th in all-play (30-33). After scoring 111.3 and 92.7 in the first two weeks, they've averaged 142.5 since. They beat friends 139.2 to 114.1 in Week 7.

### What's working

The running backs rank 3rd: Ashton Jeanty and James Cook III have 17.6 and 18.9 per start. Malik Nabers has 17.4 per start, and D/ST leads the league. Colston Loveland scored 19.9 in Week 7, his best game of the season.

### What isn't

Tight end is still last in the league (47.3), and the choice between Loveland and Mark Andrews has often gone the wrong way. The bench has scored 401.1 points: RJ Harvey has 52.7 bench points and Rhamondre Stevenson 47.7. The quarterback rotation between Jayden Daniels and Jordan Love has also left points behind.

### Outlook

Barcelona has the 5th-hardest remaining schedule. Daniels is back in the Week 8 lineup (projected 22.8), with Nabers on bye.
