# Barcelona's 181.0 is a sign of what this roster can do

Through Week 5

*A season-best week, with only 5.0 points left on the bench, and Barcelona is up to 2-3.*

Barcelona Backshotters are 2-3 and 8th in the standings, ranking 8th in points per game (128.9) and all-play (17-28). Their scoring has risen sharply: 111.3 and 92.7 in the first two weeks, then 132.7, 127.1 and 181.0. In Week 5 they beat Cleveland by 46.0.

### What's working

Jayden Daniels has 23.5 per start, including 32.3 in Week 5. James Cook III (17.9 per start) and Ashton Jeanty (17.1) have started every week. Malik Nabers and George Pickens both topped 28 in Week 5. D/ST leads the league, and kicker ranks 3rd. Lineup efficiency has climbed to 85.9% (5th).

### What isn't

Tight end ranks last in the league by a wide margin (26.0). Mark Andrews has 30.0 bench points against 13.7 in his one start, and Colston Loveland has struggled. Receiver ranks 7th for the season.

### Outlook

Barcelona has the 2nd-hardest remaining schedule (tied). Jordan Love is starting at quarterback in Week 6, with Daniels on the bench. If the last three weeks are the real team, Barcelona can get back to .500.
