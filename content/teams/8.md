# Barcelona is more competitive, but still 1-3

Through Week 4

*Scores of 132.7 and 127.1 in the last two weeks are a clear improvement, and the bench is still costing games.*

Barcelona Backshotters are 1-3 and 9th in the standings, ranking 9th in points per game (115.9) and all-play (9-27). After scoring 111.3 and 92.7 in the first two weeks, they've scored 132.7 and 127.1 since. They lost Week 4 to Fraudulent Failures, 137.2 to 127.1.

### What's working

The D/ST leads the league (44.0). Ashton Jeanty (18.7 per start) and James Cook III (16.6) have started every week. Sam Darnold has 20.5 per start in two starts. Malik Nabers scored 23.2 in Week 4, his best game, and Mark Andrews 13.7 in his first start.

### What isn't

Tight end still ranks last (22.1). Receivers rank 7th: Nabers, Jaylen Waddle and George Pickens all average between 10 and 11.2 per start. The bench has scored 261.4 points: RJ Harvey has 38.1 without a start, and Xavier Worthy 34.3. In Week 4, Barcelona's best possible lineup would have scored 153.3 and won. Saquon Barkley has 11.0 points in two starts.

### Outlook

Barcelona has the 2nd-hardest remaining schedule. Jayden Daniels is back as the Week 5 starter. The roster can compete; the lineup choices need to catch up.
