# Cleveland's receivers are elite, and the rest is a work in progress

Through Week 2

*The league's 2nd-best WR group is being held back by an injured backfield and some costly bench calls.*

Cleveland Browns are 1-1 and rank 5th in points per game (134.7), but only 7th in all-play (7-11). Their Week 1 score of 129.7 ranked 9th of 10. In Week 2 they rebounded to beat Fraudulent Failures, 139.6 to 124.6.

### What's working

The receivers. Cleveland ranks 2nd in WR production (138.8) with Chris Olave (25.4 per start), Christian Watson (46.8 over two starts, including a 32.7 on a 10.9 projection in Week 1) and Garrett Wilson (30.6). Brandon Aubrey's 19.0 ties for the 3rd-best kicker production.

### What isn't

Almost everything else. Cleveland ranks 9th at running back (49.3). De'Von Achane scored 22.9 over two starts and is now on IR, and Saquon Barkley scored 12.0 over two starts before leaving the roster. D/ST is last in the league at -3.0, thanks to a -4.0 from the Texans in Week 1.

The bench has been costly. Tyler Shough scored 45.6 points on it over two weeks, while Jared Goff (16.4) and Bryce Young (24.1) got the starts. Stefon Diggs has 37.2 bench points and Juwan Johnson 25.0, more than starting tight end Tyler Warren's 24.7. Cleveland's bench has scored 182.1 points, the most in the league, and its lineup efficiency is 82.2%.

### Outlook

Cleveland has the 2nd-easiest remaining schedule, and the receivers give them a weekly advantage. Settling on one quarterback and getting production from running back, where Chuba Hubbard is the most reliable option, would make this a playoff team.
