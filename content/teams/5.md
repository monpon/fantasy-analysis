# Cleveland's bench has outscored some teams' starters

Through Week 5

*Christian Watson scored 32.3 on the bench in Week 5, and Cleveland is 2-3.*

Cleveland Browns are 2-3 and 6th in the standings, ranking 5th in points per game (138.9) and 7th in all-play (22-23). They lost Week 5 to Barcelona's 181.0.

### What's working

Receiver leads the league by a wide margin (342.0). Chris Olave has 19.9 per start, Christian Watson 19.3 and Garrett Wilson 17.3. Parker Washington has 21.7 per start since arriving, including 30.4 in Week 5. Kyler Murray scored 18.3 in his first start at quarterback for the team.

### What isn't

The bench has scored 391.6 points, and lineup efficiency (82.6%) is last in the league. Juwan Johnson has 68.8 bench points without a start. Watson's 32.3 in Week 5 was the highest bench score of any player that week. Running back ranks 9th and D/ST 8th.

### Outlook

Cleveland is tied with Tennessee for the easiest remaining schedule in the league. With this many good receivers, start/sit decisions are the main thing standing between Cleveland and a winning record.
