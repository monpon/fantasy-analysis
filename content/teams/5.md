# Cleveland's scoring has dropped, and its bench is still the league's deepest

Through Week 7

*Two straight scores under 105, and 506.8 bench points, about 71 more than any other team.*

Cleveland Browns are 3-4 and 7th in the standings, ranking 8th in points per game (128.5) and 8th in all-play (25-38). They scored 103.6 and 101.4 in Weeks 6 and 7, splitting the two games.

### What's working

Receiver ranks 2nd (411.2). Chris Olave has 17.9 per start and Christian Watson 16.4. Chuba Hubbard has 17.1 per start, and Braelon Allen 14.8.

### What isn't

Lineup decisions. The bench has scored 506.8 points, and 185.9 have been left behind over the season. Both totals are the most in the league, and efficiency (82.9%) is last. QB (8th), RB (9th) and TE (9th) all rank in the bottom three. Juwan Johnson scored 68.8 on the bench and 0.0 in his one start, and is no longer on the roster.

### Outlook

The 9th-hardest remaining schedule is favorable. For Week 8, Geno Smith starts at quarterback, with Olave on bye.
