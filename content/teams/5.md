# Cleveland has won three straight despite the league's least efficient lineups

Through Week 9

*Christian Watson scored 30.1 in Week 9, and Cleveland moved to 5-4.*

Cleveland Browns are 5-4 and 5th in the standings, after three straight wins. They rank 7th in points per game (129.0) and 7th in all-play (34-47), and the record is 1.2 wins better than the scores predict. ESPN's simulations give them 48% playoff odds.

### What's working

The receivers rank 1st in the league. Christian Watson has 18.2 per start and Chris Olave 17.8, and Garrett Wilson has 15.0. Chuba Hubbard has 17.7 per start at running back.

### What isn't

Lineup decisions. Efficiency is 83.7%, last in the league, and the bench has scored 599.8 points, the most of any team. Bryce Young has scored 66.8 on the bench, including 20.3 in Week 9 while the starter scored 3.8. Quarterback ranks last, running back and tight end 8th, and kicker 9th.

### Outlook

Cleveland has the 7th-hardest remaining schedule. For Week 10, Young starts at quarterback and Carnell Tate at receiver, and ESPN projects Cleveland as a 41.7-point favorite over a short-handed Bagel Busters lineup.
