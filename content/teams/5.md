# Cleveland's bench could win this league

Through Week 3

*The best receivers in the league, and a bench that has scored 274.4 points, more than any other.*

Cleveland Browns are 1-2 and 7th in the standings, ranking 5th in points per game (141.2) and T-5th in all-play (13-14). Scoring has gone up every week (129.7, 139.6, 154.2), but they lost Week 3 to master(rage)baiters on a Monday night comeback.

### What's working

Receiver, by a wide margin. Cleveland leads the league in WR production (220.8): Chris Olave (23.5 per start), Christian Watson (23.1) and Garrett Wilson (19.1) have started every week. QB ranks 3rd, and Tyler Shough scored 23.8 in his first start. Brandon Aubrey ties for 2nd-best kicker production.

### What isn't

Lineup management. Cleveland's bench has scored 274.4 points, the most in the league, and lineup efficiency is 82.5% (8th). Juwan Johnson has 51.3 bench points without a start, more than starting tight end Tyler Warren (39.4). Stefon Diggs has 44.5, and Shough scored 45.6 on the bench before his first start. In Week 3 alone, Cleveland's best possible lineup would have scored 185.3 and won easily.

Running back ranks 9th. De'Von Achane has 8.2 per start and is now on IR. D/ST ranks last (4.0).

### Outlook

The 2nd-easiest remaining schedule is a real opportunity. With the best receivers in the league, fixing the start/sit decisions, especially Johnson at TE, would make Cleveland dangerous.
