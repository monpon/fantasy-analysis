# Cleveland gets back to .500, but the bench keeps outscoring the lineup

Through Week 4

*The league's best receivers and a bench that has scored 348.3 points, the most of any team.*

Cleveland Browns are 2-2 and 6th in the standings, ranking 6th in points per game (139.9) and 5th in all-play (19-17). They beat GDID 135.9 to 110.8 in Week 4.

### What's working

Receiver leads the league (270.3). Chris Olave has 22.5 per start, Christian Watson 19.3 and Garrett Wilson 15.8. New tight end T.J. Hockenson scored 24.9 in his first start. Chuba Hubbard has 18.4 per start, and kicker ranks 2nd.

### What isn't

The bench has scored 348.3 points, the most in the league, and lineup efficiency is 82.5%. Juwan Johnson has 63.2 bench points without a start and Stefon Diggs 53.0. At quarterback, the benched player has outscored the starter in two of four weeks. In Week 4, Bryce Young scored 21.5 on the bench while Tyler Shough started for 15.9. Running back ranks 9th, with De'Von Achane and Travis Etienne Jr. both on IR. D/ST ranks last (5.0).

### Outlook

The remaining schedule is 5th-hardest. Week 5 is complicated by the Carolina bye, which takes Hubbard and Young off the field. Cleveland's talent is clear; the challenge is choosing which players to start.
