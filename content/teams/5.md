# Cleveland is back to .500 with the league's best receivers

Through Week 8

*Cleveland won with nine starters in Week 8, and its D/ST slot is empty again for Week 9.*

Cleveland Browns are 4-4 and 5th in the standings. They rank 6th in points per game (129.9) and T-6th in all-play (31-41), and the record is about 0.6 wins better than the scores predict. In Week 8 they beat i hate darius cooper 139.8 to 136.2 without a D/ST in the lineup.

### What's working

Receiver leads the league (469.6). Chris Olave (17.9 per start), Christian Watson (16.5), Garrett Wilson (15.0) and Michael Wilson (13.9) give them unmatched depth. Chuba Hubbard (18.4 per start) and Braelon Allen (16.5) have been solid at running back.

### What isn't

Quarterback ranks last in the league after a long rotation of starters. Lineup efficiency (83.7%) is last, and the bench has scored 553.9 points, the most of any team. TE ranks 9th.

### Outlook

Cleveland has the easiest remaining schedule in the league. For Week 9, the lineup again has no D/ST, and its best projected lineup is 11.4 points better than the current one.
