# Net Worth: 1.4b is in first place, and its record is ahead of its scores

Through Week 5

*Four straight wins and sole possession of first, with the 7th-highest scoring average in the league.*

Net Worth: 1.4b is 4-1 and alone in 1st place after beating poverty 146.0 to 137.9. But it ranks 7th in points per game (137.5) and 5th in all-play (24-21), and its record is 1.3 wins better than its scores predict, the biggest such gap in the league.

### What's working

Jaxon Smith-Njigba has 140.5 points (28.1 per start), third in the league behind Jahmyr Gibbs and Josh Allen. Brock Bowers has 23.7 per start since entering the lineup in Week 3, and Jared Goff 22.9. Receiver ranks 3rd, tight end T-2nd and D/ST 3rd. Will Reichard, added at kicker, scored 16.0 against his former team in Week 5.

### What isn't

Running back ranks last in the league (121.1). Cam Skattebo has 10.3 per start, and Tony Pollard 4.8 in two starts. Deebo Samuel is now listed out, and Jaxson Dart remains on IR. Jalen Coker has 48.4 bench points against 3.8 in his one start.

### Outlook

The 6th-hardest remaining schedule is middling. Week 6 is complicated by byes: Goff, Aaron Jones Sr. and Reichard are all off, with Jameis Winston starting at quarterback. Running back is still the clearest need.
