# Bowers arrives, and THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING finds another gear

Through Week 3

*A 162.9 in Week 3, led by the league's best player, has this team at 2-1 and 4th in all-play.*

THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING is 2-1 and 4th in the standings. It ranks 6th in points per game (138.8) and 4th in all-play (15-12). After 130.5 and 123.1 in the first two weeks, it scored 162.9 in Week 3 and beat Tennessee by 56.2.

### What's working

Jaxon Smith-Njigba is the highest-scoring player in the league: 104.1 points, 34.7 per start, and at least 26 every week. Drake London added 29.4 in Week 3. Brock Bowers scored 27.6 in his first start after two scoreless weeks on the bench, which could change the tight end spot. The team ranks 2nd at receiver, kicker and D/ST.

### What isn't

Running back ranks last in the league (65.7). Cam Skattebo (12.2 per start) and Aaron Jones Sr. (12.3) have been ordinary, and Rachaad White has 30.1 bench points without a start. QB ranks 9th. Jaxson Dart went on IR after scoring 26.6 and 0.8, and Jared Goff scored 19.4 in his first start.

Jalen Coker's timing has been rough: 48.4 points on the bench in the first two weeks, then 3.8 in his first start. Deebo Samuel has 39.9 bench points.

### Outlook

The 4th-hardest remaining schedule is a challenge. If Bowers is a regular starter from here, the top of this lineup matches anyone's. The priority is running back, and the receiver depth (Deebo, Coker) is the obvious trade material.
