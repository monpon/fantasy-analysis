# Smith-Njigba is a star; THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING needs a supporting cast

Through Week 2

*One of the league's best players, the top kicker and the top defense, and bottom-three production at QB, RB and TE.*

THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING are 1-1 and rank 7th in points per game (126.8) and all-play (7-11). They lost Week 1 to GDID, 142.9 to 130.5, then beat Barcelona 123.1 to 92.7.

### What's working

Jaxon Smith-Njigba has averaged 34.4 per start, 2nd only to Josh Allen among players with two starts, including the week's best score of 42.5 in Week 2. He's the main reason this team ranks 3rd in WR production. They also lead the league at kicker (Harrison Butker, 24.0) and D/ST (Steelers, 27.0).

### What isn't

The rest of the lineup. Running back ranks last in the league (38.5): Cam Skattebo has 23.6 over two starts, and Aaron Jones Sr. and Tony Pollard have added little. QB ranks 9th: Jaxson Dart scored 26.6 in Week 1, 0.8 in Week 2, and is now on IR. Tight end ranks 8th.

Then there's the bench. Jalen Coker has scored 48.4 points there without a start, the most bench points of any player in the league, including 33.8 in Week 1. Deebo Samuel (24.5) and Rachaad White (19.0) have also scored on the bench. Brock Bowers scored 0.0 on the bench in both weeks.

### Outlook

This team faces the 2nd-hardest remaining schedule. Trading from its receiver depth for a running back, and getting Coker into the lineup, are the clearest paths to more wins.
