# Net Worth: 1.4b is 3-1, and the scoreboard says that's generous

Through Week 4

*The renamed team has won three straight, but its all-play record is exactly .500.*

Net Worth: 1.4b (formerly THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING) is 3-1 and 3rd in the standings, after three straight wins. But it ranks 7th in points per game (135.4) and T-6th in all-play (18-18). The record is a full win better than the scores predict, the biggest such gap in the league.

### What's working

Jaxon Smith-Njigba leads the league in total points (116.7, 29.2 per start). Brock Bowers has 24.1 per start since joining the lineup in Week 3. Receiver ranks 3rd and D/ST 3rd. In Week 4, the lineup won a close one, 125.0 to 121.7, with five starters scoring 14 or more.

### What isn't

Running back ranks last in the league (90.1). Cam Skattebo (11.1 per start) and Aaron Jones Sr. (13.8) have been ordinary, and Tony Pollard (34.1) and Rachaad White (30.1) have scored mostly on the bench. Quarterback ranks 8th: Jaxson Dart is on IR, and Jared Goff has 19.9 per start. Jalen Coker (48.4) and Deebo Samuel (39.9) have big bench totals.

### Outlook

The 4th-hardest remaining schedule will test whether the record holds. With Smith-Njigba and Bowers, the top of the lineup is as good as anyone's. Running back is still the clear need.
