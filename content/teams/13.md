# poverty's perfect start is built on one enormous week

Through Week 2

*The league's top scorer is 2-0, but take away the 192.5 and this is a good team, not a dominant one.*

poverty leads the league at 161.2 points per game, and that number is real: a 192.5 in Week 2 was the highest score of the season, and it buried Tennessee by 77.6. But the all-play record tells a more measured story. At 11-7, poverty ranks 4th. Week 1's 130.0 ranked 8th of 10 and would have beaten only two teams. The 2-0 record is about 0.8 wins better than their weekly scores would predict.

### What's working

The core is excellent. Jonathan Taylor has averaged 27.1 per start, CeeDee Lamb 25.4, and DeVonta Smith has added 37.0 over two starts. Dalton Schultz's 26.0 in his only start gave the tight end spot a boost, and the Seahawks D/ST (24.0) rank 2nd in the league. poverty's lineup efficiency, 89.5%, is 2nd-best in the league.

### What isn't

The quarterback call went wrong in Week 1: Joe Burrow started (14.2) while Patrick Mahomes scored 21.7 on the bench. They got it right in Week 2, when Mahomes scored 29.0. DJ Moore has been boom or bust (21.0, then -0.1). Kyle Pitts Sr. scored 0.0 in his one start. And the roster carries dead weight: Adonai Mitchell and Tyreek Hill are listed out, and Josh Jacobs hasn't scored yet.

### Outlook

With the 7th-hardest remaining schedule, poverty is well placed. The question is whether the Week 2 ceiling or the Week 1 floor is the real team. Settling on one quarterback and freeing up roster spots held by inactive players would help.
