# poverty is 2-1, and the 192.5 is looking like the exception

Through Week 3

*After a 107.2 loss to Washington, poverty's all-play record has fallen below .500.*

poverty is 2-1 and 3rd in the standings, and ranks 4th in points per game (143.2). But that average rests heavily on Week 2's 192.5, the best score of the season. The other two weeks were 130.0 and 107.2, both of which ranked 8th in the league that week. The all-play record is 13-14, and the 2-1 record is about 0.6 wins better than the scores predict.

### What's working

CeeDee Lamb has been consistent (23.6 per start), and Jonathan Taylor has averaged 21.2. Patrick Mahomes has 22.5 per start. Kicker and D/ST rank in the top three, and lineup efficiency (88.1%) is 3rd.

### What isn't

The quarterback decision has gone wrong in two of three weeks. Burrow started Week 1 (14.2) while Mahomes scored 21.7 on the bench, and Mahomes started Week 3 (15.9) while Burrow scored 22.6 on the bench. Burrow now has 38.8 bench points. Jakobi Meyers has 36.4 bench points without a start, more than starting receivers DJ Moore (34.6) and Devaughn Vele (18.3 in starts).

Tight end ranks 8th. Taylor (9.2) and Bucky Irving (7.8) both came in well under projection in Week 3. Adonai Mitchell and Tyreek Hill are listed out, and Josh Jacobs hasn't scored yet.

### Outlook

The 5th-hardest remaining schedule is middling. poverty has the ceiling to beat anyone. Settling on one QB and getting Meyers into the lineup would raise the floor.
