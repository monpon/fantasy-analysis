# poverty takes first place behind CeeDee Lamb

Through Week 4

*A 149.0 win in Week 4 put poverty alone in 1st, but the quarterback decision keeps going wrong.*

poverty is 3-1 and in 1st place. It ranks 2nd in points per game (144.7) and 4th in all-play (21-15), and its record is about 0.7 wins better than its scores predict. Weekly scores have swung widely: 130.0, 192.5, 107.2, 149.0.

### What's working

CeeDee Lamb has 28.0 per start, second among receivers only to Jaxon Smith-Njigba, including 41.3 in Week 4. Jonathan Taylor (21.6 per start) has started every week. The Seahawks D/ST (40.0) ranks 2nd, and lineup efficiency (88.7%) ties for 2nd. Tucker Kraft scored 17.5 in his first start at tight end.

### What isn't

Quarterback. The benched QB has outscored the starter in three of four weeks, and Joe Burrow now has 62.5 bench points against 14.2 in his one start. Running back ranks 8th: Bucky Irving has 11.8 per start. Tight end also ranks 8th. DJ Moore has 9.3 per start, and Jakobi Meyers has 36.4 bench points.

### Outlook

poverty has the 2nd-easiest remaining schedule of the ten teams. Week 5 is complicated by the bye: Patrick Mahomes and Kansas City are off, so Burrow is starting. With Lamb and Taylor carrying the offense, a consistent quarterback decision would make this the team to beat.
