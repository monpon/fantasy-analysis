# Tennessee has won five straight and leads the league in scoring

Through Week 8

*6-2, 1st in points per game, and a 159.8 in Week 8 despite leaving 48.5 points on the bench.*

Tennessee Top G's are 6-2 and in 1st place, after five straight wins. They lead the league in points per game (146.4) and are T-2nd in all-play (43-29). The record is still 1.2 wins better than the scores predict, partly because the Week 6 and 7 wins came with ordinary scores.

### What's working

The receivers rank 3rd: Tee Higgins has 22.6 per start, including 37.5 in Week 8, and Amon-Ra St. Brown 20.1. Kyren Williams (18.9 per start) and Jaylen Warren (18.6) give them a solid backfield. Drake Maye has scored 22.5 or more in five of his last six games.

### What isn't

The bench. Tennessee's bench has scored 448.6 points, and 169.5 have been left behind over the season. In Week 8 alone, Tyler Allgeier scored 36.1 and Emmett Johnson 26.3 on the bench. The tight end choice has gone wrong in five of eight weeks. D/ST ranks 7th.

### Outlook

The 4th-hardest remaining schedule is a real test. For Week 9, Warren is on bye with Pittsburgh, and Allgeier (9.3) is in the lineup.
