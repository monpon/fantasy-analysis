# Tennessee has won six straight and is alone in first

Through Week 9

*7-2, 1st in points per game, and the best playoff odds in the league.*

Tennessee Top G's are 7-2 and in 1st place after six straight wins. They lead the league in points per game (144.9) and rank 3rd in all-play (48-33). The record is 1.7 wins better than the scores predict, the most in the league. ESPN's simulations give them 98% playoff odds.

### What's working

The receivers rank 2nd: Tee Higgins has 22.2 per start and Amon-Ra St. Brown 19.8. Drake Maye has 19.3, and Jaylen Warren (18.6) and Kyren Williams (17.8) give them a reliable backfield. In Week 9, no starter scored more than 20.2 and they still won by 17.8.

### What isn't

The bench has scored 489.3 points, and the tight end choice keeps going the wrong way: in Week 9, Isaiah Likely scored 16.3 on the bench while Harold Fannin Jr. started for 12.2. D/ST ranks 8th.

### Outlook

Tennessee has the 3rd-hardest remaining schedule. For Week 10, Warren is back, Likely starts at tight end, and ESPN projects a 24.0-point win over Washington.
