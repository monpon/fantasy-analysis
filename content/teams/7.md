# Tennessee has the league's best receivers and problems everywhere else

Through Week 2

*St. Brown and Jefferson lead the top WR group, but Tennessee ranks bottom-three at QB, RB, TE and D/ST.*

Tennessee Top G's are 1-1 and rank 6th in points per game (128.6) and all-play (8-10). They opened with a 142.2 win over Washington, then lost 192.5 to 114.9 to poverty in the biggest blowout of the season.

### What's working

The receivers, clearly the best in the league (148.2 points). Amon-Ra St. Brown has averaged 32.0 per start. Justin Jefferson scored 31.2 in Week 1, Nico Collins 21.2, and Tee Higgins has added 23.4 over two starts. Tyler Loop gives them the 2nd-best kicker production.

### What isn't

Quarterback, by a wide margin. Drake Maye has 17.8 points over two starts, 8.9 per game, the lowest QB production in the league. Running back ranks 8th: Jadarian Price has 12.8 over two starts, while Jaylen Warren scored 10.3 on the bench in Week 1.

At tight end, Tennessee has picked the wrong player both weeks. Week 1: Harold Fannin Jr. started (4.1), Isaiah Likely scored 27.8 on the bench. Week 2: Likely started (8.3), Fannin scored 10.4 on the bench. The Rams D/ST scored 12.0 on the bench while the Chargers D/ST started for 4.0.

Collins is now listed out, and Jefferson fell from 31.2 to 8.5.

### Outlook

The 5th-hardest remaining schedule offers no break. The receivers can win weeks on their own, but only if quarterback improves. That's the one position where an upgrade would change this team the most.
