# Tennessee is trending the wrong way

Through Week 3

*Tennessee's scoring has dropped every week, and quarterback remains the worst position group in the league.*

Tennessee Top G's are 1-2 and 8th in the standings, ranking 7th in points per game (121.3) and 8th in all-play (9-18). Scoring has fallen from 142.2 to 114.9 to 106.8, and the last two losses were by 77.6 and 56.2.

### What's working

The receivers still rank 3rd (186.3). Amon-Ra St. Brown has averaged 25.3 per start, Justin Jefferson 15.0 and Tee Higgins 14.8. Kyren Williams has been steady (17.7 per start), and Tyler Loop leads the league in kicker production (33.0).

### What isn't

Quarterback, by a wide margin: Drake Maye has 22.6 points in three starts, 7.5 per game, the lowest QB production in the league. Running back ranks 8th, with Jadarian Price at 4.7 per start.

Tight end has gone wrong every week. The benched player outscored the starter in Week 1 (Isaiah Likely 27.8 over Harold Fannin Jr. 4.1), Week 2 (Fannin 10.4 over Likely 8.3) and Week 3 (Fannin 24.1 over Likely 3.3). TE ranks 9th and D/ST 8th.

Nico Collins is listed out, and Jefferson has scored 8.5 and 5.2 the last two weeks.

### Outlook

The 6th-hardest remaining schedule offers little relief. The fastest improvement is at quarterback. At tight end, Fannin (38.6) and Likely (39.4) have nearly identical season totals, but the one on the bench has outscored the starter every week.
