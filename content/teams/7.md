# Tennessee's 207.1 resets its season

Through Week 4

*After three straight declines, Tennessee scored the most points of the season and is back to .500.*

Tennessee Top G's are 2-2 and 5th in the standings. They rank 5th in points per game (142.7) and T-6th in all-play (18-18). After scoring 142.2, 114.9 and 106.8, Tennessee scored 207.1 in Week 4, the highest score of the season, and beat The Bagel Busters by 84.4.

### What's working

The receivers rank 2nd (259.3). Amon-Ra St. Brown has 22.8 per start, Tee Higgins 17.8, and Nico Collins 26.0 in two starts. Kyren Williams has 22.7 per start, with 37.7 in Week 4. Tyler Loop leads the league in kicker production. Lineup efficiency (88.7%) ties for 2nd.

### What isn't

Quarterback still ranks last. Drake Maye has 7.5 per start, and his best game, 26.2 in Week 4, came on the bench while C.J. Stroud started and scored 23.1. Stroud is no longer on the roster, and Maye starts in Week 5. Tight end ranks 9th, and the benched player has outscored the starter in all four weeks.

### Outlook

The 6th-hardest remaining schedule is middling. Week 4 showed what this team can do when the receivers and running backs all hit at once. Quarterback remains the position that most limits it.
