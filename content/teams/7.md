# Tennessee has won four straight, with some help from the schedule

Through Week 7

*5-2 and 2nd in points per game, but 1.2 wins ahead of what the all-play record predicts.*

Tennessee Top G's are 5-2 and 2nd in the standings, with four straight wins. They rank 2nd in points per game (144.5) but 5th in all-play (34-29). The record is 1.2 wins better than the scores predict, the biggest positive gap in the league. Weeks 6 and 7 were wins with 113.0 and 131.4, scores that ranked 7th and 6th those weeks.

### What's working

Drake Maye has been a different player since Week 4: after averaging 7.5 over his first three starts, he's scored 26.2 (on the bench), 39.5, 23.8 and 27.4. Amon-Ra St. Brown has 21.4 per start, Kyren Williams 19.7, Tee Higgins 19.6 and Jaylen Warren 18.2. Lineup efficiency (89.3%) ranks 2nd.

### What isn't

Tight end. The benched tight end has outscored the starter in five of seven weeks, and Harold Fannin Jr. now has 50.9 bench points. D/ST ranks T-8th, and Nico Collins scored 2.6 in Week 7.

### Outlook

Tennessee faces the 2nd-hardest remaining schedule. The high-end talent is real, but the last two wins came with ordinary scores.
