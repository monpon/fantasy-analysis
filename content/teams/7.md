# Tennessee is now the league's highest-scoring team

Through Week 5

*Back-to-back scores of 207.1 and 195.8 have taken Tennessee from 1-2 to 3-2 and 1st in points per game.*

Tennessee Top G's are 3-2 and 2nd in the standings. Two straight huge weeks have moved them to 1st in points per game (153.3) and 3rd in all-play (27-18). Their record matches their scores. In Week 5 they beat Fraudulent Failures 195.8 to 159.9.

### What's working

Nearly everything recently. Drake Maye scored 39.5 in Week 5 after averaging 7.5 over his first three starts. Kyren Williams has 22.1 per start, Amon-Ra St. Brown 20.8, Jaylen Warren 20.7 and Nico Collins 20.9. Receiver ranks 2nd. Lineup efficiency (90.2%) ranks 2nd, and Isaiah Likely's 32.4 in Week 5 was the first time the starting tight end outscored the benched one.

### What isn't

Quarterback still ranks last for the season (85.1) because of the slow start, and tight end ranks 8th. In Week 6, St. Brown is in the lineup during Detroit's bye, projected for 0, and with Justin Jefferson and Tee Higgins also on bye, there's no healthy receiver on the bench to replace him.

### Outlook

Tennessee is tied with Cleveland for the easiest remaining schedule in the league. If the last two weeks are the real team, they're the favorite. Week 6 byes make this a tougher week than usual.
