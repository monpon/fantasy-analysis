# The Bagel Busters keep starting the wrong quarterback

Through Week 7

*Dak Prescott has 78.5 bench points, the most of any player in the league.*

The Bagel Busters are 2-5 and last in the standings, ranking 9th in points per game (116.5) and last in all-play (17-46). Week 6 brought the lowest score of the season (84.6), and Week 7 their second-best (136.8), which still lost.

### What's working

Puka Nacua has 22.9 per start. Jameson Williams (15.0 per start) and Tre Tucker (14.4) have improved, and both topped 21 in Week 7. Josh Jacobs scored 20.1 in his first start. Lineup efficiency (87.7%) ranks 4th.

### What isn't

Quarterback. The team carries Dak Prescott, Matthew Stafford and Caleb Williams, and the benched quarterbacks keep outscoring the starter. Prescott has 78.5 bench points, the most of any player in the league, and in Week 7 both Prescott (23.3) and Williams (22.1) outscored the starter, Stafford (17.5). Derrick Henry has scored 5.0 and 5.9 in the last two weeks. D/ST ranks last.

### Outlook

The Bagel Busters have the 6th-hardest remaining schedule. Stafford starts again in Week 8 (16.9), with Prescott on the bench projected higher (19.5).
