# The Bagel Busters are out of last place behind Nacua

Through Week 5

*Puka Nacua has 26.3 per start since returning, and the Bagel Busters beat Washington.*

The Bagel Busters are 2-3 and 9th in the standings. They rank 9th in points per game (118.9) and T-9th in all-play (12-33), so the 2-3 record is about 0.7 wins better than the scores predict. In Week 5 they beat Washington 135.8 to 118.7, their best score since Week 1.

### What's working

Puka Nacua has 26.3 per start, including 37.7 in Week 5. Derrick Henry has 22.4 per start and has started every week. Brenton Strange scored 13.8 at tight end in Week 5.

### What isn't

The quarterback decision keeps going wrong. Matthew Stafford has 57.9 bench points without a start, including 27.3 in Week 5 while Dak Prescott started (12.6). QB ranks 8th, running back 8th and kicker 9th. Breece Hall and Will Shipley are listed doubtful.

### Outlook

The Bagel Busters face the 4th-hardest remaining schedule (tied). For Week 6, Stafford is starting (projected 19.6). With Nacua and Henry, the top of the lineup can compete with anyone.
