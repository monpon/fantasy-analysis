# Injuries and a Week 2 collapse put The Bagel Busters in a hole

Through Week 2

*The Bagel Busters scored 142.3 in Week 1, then 91.2, and two of their best players are now listed out.*

The Bagel Busters are 1-1 but rank 9th in points per game (116.7) and all-play (6-12). They beat Cleveland 142.3 to 129.7 in Week 1, then lost the league's lowest-scoring game to GDID, 99.2 to 91.2.

### What's working

Derrick Henry has averaged 26.5 per start. Breece Hall has added 34.0 over two starts. The team ranks 3rd at quarterback, 2nd at tight end and 3rd at D/ST (49ers, 18.0).

### What isn't

The receivers rank 9th (47.2). Puka Nacua scored 13.4 in his one start and is now listed out. Jameson Williams (13.8 over two starts), Terry McLaurin (10.4) and Courtland Sutton (9.6) haven't produced. Tre Tucker has scored 27.6 on the bench. The kicker, Cameron Dicker, has 3.0 points over two starts, last in the league.

The Week 2 quarterback decision cost them a win: Caleb Williams started and scored 7.7 while Dak Prescott scored 29.8 on the bench. With their best possible lineup, 135.4, they would have beaten GDID easily. Lineup efficiency is 81.2%, 8th in the league.

Caleb Williams and Nacua are now listed out, and Jordan Mason and Jordyn Tyson are on IR.

### Outlook

The 4th-hardest remaining schedule makes this tougher. Prescott is the obvious starter now, and a kicker change is the easiest fix. The receiving group needs help until Nacua returns.
