# The Bagel Busters are in last place and need help fast

Through Week 3

*Two straight losses, the week's lowest score, and two of their best players listed out.*

The Bagel Busters are 1-2 and last in the standings, ranking 10th in points per game (111.9) and 9th in all-play (6-21). After a 142.3 win in Week 1, they scored 91.2 and 102.2, the lowest score in the league in both weeks.

### What's working

Derrick Henry (25.0 per start) has been reliable. Tight end ranks 3rd, with Jake Ferguson at 14.8 per start. Terry McLaurin scored 19.7 in Week 3 after 10.4 over the first two weeks. Dak Prescott scored 18.9 in his first start.

### What isn't

Lineup decisions: efficiency is 80.9%, last in the league. In Week 2 they started Caleb Williams (7.7) over Prescott (29.8). In Week 3 they started Oronde Gadsden at FLEX (0.0) while Matthew Stafford (20.9), Keenan Allen (18.3) and Malik Washington (11.1) scored on the bench. Tre Tucker has 27.6 bench points.

Receiver ranks 9th and kicker is last (Cameron Dicker, 4.0 per start). Caleb Williams and Puka Nacua are listed out, and Jordan Mason and Jordyn Tyson are on IR.

### Outlook

The 3rd-hardest remaining schedule makes the climb harder. Prescott is the starter, Allen deserves a lineup spot, and a new kicker is the easiest upgrade available. Nacua's return timeline will shape the season.
