# The Bagel Busters are scoring better and out of last place

Through Week 8

*Dak Prescott started and led the team's quarterbacks in Week 8, and the Bagel Busters climbed out of last.*

The Bagel Busters are 3-5 and 9th in the standings, ranking 9th in points per game (119.7) and all-play (24-48). Their record matches their scores. Scores of 136.8 and 142.1 in the last two weeks are two of their three best of the season.

### What's working

Puka Nacua has 23.3 per start. Jameson Williams (15.5 per start) has become a reliable second receiver, and Josh Jacobs scored 20.1 and 23.2 in his first two starts. Lineup efficiency (88.6%) ranks 2nd in the league.

### What isn't

Running back ranks 9th. Derrick Henry has 17.1 per start but has scored 5.0, 5.9 and 14.0 in the last three weeks. Tight end, kicker and D/ST all rank 8th. Over the season, the benched quarterback has outscored the starter four times.

### Outlook

The Bagel Busters have the 2nd-easiest remaining schedule. For Week 9, Chris Boswell is in the lineup at kicker while Pittsburgh is on bye.
