# Nacua is back, but The Bagel Busters are still in last place

Through Week 4

*A near-perfect lineup in Week 4 still lost by 84.4 to the season's highest score.*

The Bagel Busters are 1-3 and last in the standings, ranking 10th in both points per game (114.6) and all-play (8-28). In Week 4 they set a lineup within 1.2 points of the best possible, the smallest gap of any team, and still lost to Tennessee's 207.1.

### What's working

Puka Nacua is back: 27.7 in Week 4 after missing two weeks. Derrick Henry has 22.7 per start and has started every week. Quarterback ranks 3rd, with Dak Prescott at 18.5 per start. The lineup management has improved: efficiency is now 85.0% (6th) after Week 4's near-perfect week.

### What isn't

Receiver ranks 8th and kicker last (Cameron Dicker, 6.0 per start). Breece Hall is listed doubtful and Caleb Williams out, and Jordan Mason and Jordyn Tyson are on IR. Tre Tucker has 8.4 per start, and Courtland Sutton 4.8.

### Outlook

The Bagel Busters face the hardest remaining schedule in the league. With Nacua and Henry healthy, the top of the lineup is good enough to win some weeks. Improving the WR2 spot and the kicker are the clearest upgrades.
