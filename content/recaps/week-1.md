# Opening weekend: Fraudulent Failures set the bar

The season opened with a league average of 137.8 points and no game closer than 10.5. Fraudulent Failures posted the high score, 174.3, and Barcelona Backshotters the low, 111.3. The best individual score of the week was Caleb Williams's 37.3.

## Fraudulent Failures hand master(rage)baiters a rough opening-week lesson

*master(rage)baiters scored the second-most points in the league and still lost by 30.7.*

@matchup 1 6

There was no bad luck to blame here. It was the other team having the best day in the league. Fraudulent Failures scored 174.3, the top score of Week 1, even though Ja'Marr Chase scored 3.2 on a 19.9 projection.

Their backfield carried them. Kenneth Walker III ran up 34.1 points, well over double his 14.7 projection, and D'Andre Swift added 32.4 on a 12.3 projection. Zay Flowers (26.0), Lamar Jackson (25.0) and Trey McBride (24.5) all topped 24 points. The only worry was depth management: Dallas Goedert scored 23.7 and Kyle Monangai 20.4 on the bench, and the team left 45.7 points there, more than anyone else in the league. They barely needed them.

master(rage)baiters did nearly everything right. Josh Allen threw up 35.7 points, Jahmyr Gibbs 33.6 and David Montgomery 28.9, and the team left only 1.8 points on the bench, the best lineup management in the league. Their 143.6 would have beaten any other team in the league. Even a perfect lineup, 145.4, wouldn't have come close.

**Next up:** Fraudulent Failures face Cleveland Browns; master(rage)baiters face The Washington football team.

## St. Brown, Jefferson and Collins lead Tennessee past Washington

*Tennessee's starting receivers outscored Washington's 90.0 to 13.4, and that was the game.*

@matchup 7 10

Tennessee Top G's won 142.2 to 131.7 with a performance from their receivers that Washington couldn't match. Justin Jefferson scored 31.2 on a 17.1 projection, Amon-Ra St. Brown 28.7 and Nico Collins 21.2. Add Tee Higgins's 8.9 and Tennessee's receivers totaled 90.0. Washington's two starting receivers, Davante Adams (5.6 on a 13.8 projection) and Carnell Tate (7.8), combined for 13.4.

Washington started four running backs, using both FLEX spots on the backfield, and got a solid 24.2 from Javonte Williams plus 24.7 from Jalen Hurts. But the choice hurt: Matthew Golden (15.5) and Denzel Boston (13.9) outscored both starting receivers on the bench. Their best possible lineup would have scored 152.5, enough to win.

Tennessee left points on the bench too. Isaiah Likely scored 27.8 there while starting TE Harold Fannin Jr. managed 4.1, and Drake Maye scored 9.8 at quarterback on a 16.3 projection. The receivers made up for all of it.

**Next up:** Tennessee Top G's face poverty; The Washington football team faces master(rage)baiters.

## Caleb Williams leads The Bagel Busters past Cleveland

*Williams's 37.3 was the best individual score of the week, and Derrick Henry added 35.3.*

@matchup 11 5

The Bagel Busters won 142.3 to 129.7 on the strength of two huge games. Caleb Williams scored 37.3, more than double his 16.4 projection, and Derrick Henry scored 35.3, more than double his 16.4. Breece Hall added 19.8. Together they covered for a quiet day from the receivers. Puka Nacua led the group with 13.4 on a 21.1 projection, and Terry McLaurin (3.4), Courtland Sutton (4.1) and Jameson Williams (8.5) combined for 16.0.

Cleveland had the biggest overperformance of the week: Christian Watson scored 32.7 on a 10.9 projection, three times what was expected. Chris Olave added 28.2. The problems were elsewhere. Saquon Barkley scored 9.0 on a 17.7 projection, De'Von Achane 10.6 on 18.2, and the Texans D/ST scored -4.0.

The bigger issue was the bench. Chuba Hubbard scored 23.7 there, Tyler Shough 23.2 and Stefon Diggs 15.5. Cleveland's best possible lineup, 162.2, would have been the week's second-highest score. Instead they finished 9th of 10.

**Next up:** The Bagel Busters face GDID; Cleveland Browns face Fraudulent Failures.

## poverty wins a low-scoring game against Barcelona

*Neither team broke out in a 130.0 to 111.3 game, but poverty's running backs did enough.*

@matchup 13 8

This wasn't a high-scoring game. poverty's 130.0 ranked 8th in the league, and Barcelona's 111.3 was last. Jonathan Taylor (25.1), DJ Moore (21.0 on an 11.6 projection) and Bucky Irving (20.3) supplied most of poverty's points, and the Seahawks D/ST added 14.0. They got 0.0 from Kyle Pitts Sr. and started Joe Burrow (14.2) while Patrick Mahomes scored 21.7 on the bench.

Barcelona's Ashton Jeanty scored 32.7, and the Jaguars D/ST added 14.0, but almost everyone else came in under projection. Jaylen Waddle scored 1.2 on a 12.2 projection, George Pickens 5.8 on 14.7, and Colston Loveland 0.0 on 12.0. Parker Washington scored 19.3 on the bench, and Jayden Daniels scored 17.7 there while Justin Herbert started at quarterback and scored 13.3.

Barcelona's best possible lineup would have scored 145.0 and won this game.

**Next up:** poverty face Tennessee Top G's; Barcelona Backshotters face THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING.

## GDID wins, and a 33.8-point bench score haunts THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING

*The winning score was 142.9. THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING's best possible lineup would have scored 175.2.*

@matchup 4 9

GDID won 142.9 to 130.5, the third-highest score of the week, behind Bijan Robinson's 31.3 and Trevor Lawrence's 26.1. Ladd McConkey (19.2) and Chase Brown (18.8) filled in the rest. A.J. Brown scored 5.6 on a 14.2 projection, and Dalton Kincaid's 18.0 sat on the bench, but neither mattered.

THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING will regret this one. Jalen Coker scored 33.8 on their bench, the highest bench score of any player in the league this week. Deebo Samuel scored 18.0 there too. Meanwhile, starters Drake London (5.5 on a 13.8 projection), Tony Pollard (4.4) and Rome Odunze (7.2) all came in low.

There were bright spots: Jaxson Dart scored 26.6, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 26.2 and the Steelers D/ST 19.0. But the math is harsh. Their best possible lineup, 175.2, would have been the highest score in the league. They left 44.7 points on the bench.

**Next up:** GDID face The Bagel Busters; THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING face Barcelona Backshotters.
