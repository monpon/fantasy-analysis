# Week 1 recaps: Fraudulent Failures open with a statement

The season opened with a league average of 137.8 points. Fraudulent Failures posted the high score (174.3), Barcelona Backshotters the low (111.3). No game was decided by less than 10.5 points. The week's best starter was a quarterback on a team that has since had to replace him: Caleb Williams scored 37.3 for The Bagel Busters.

## Fraudulent Failures 174.3, master(rage)baiters 143.6: the week's second-best score runs into the best

master(rage)baiters did nearly everything right and still lost by 30.7. Their 143.6 was the second-highest score of the week and would have beaten eight of the nine teams in the league. They also managed their lineup almost perfectly, leaving just 1.8 points on the bench. Josh Allen (35.7 against a 19.3 projection), Jahmyr Gibbs (33.6) and David Montgomery (28.9) all delivered. Even their best possible lineup, 145.4, wouldn't have come close.

Fraudulent Failures simply had a bigger day. Kenneth Walker III scored 34.1 and D'Andre Swift 32.4, which was more than double his 12.3 projection, and Zay Flowers added 26.0. Remarkably, they did it with Ja'Marr Chase scoring 3.2 against a 19.9 projection and 45.7 points left on the bench, the most of any team this week. Dallas Goedert scored 23.7 on the bench. It was the week's top score with room to spare.

## Tennessee Top G's 142.2, The Washington football team 131.7: Tennessee's receivers carry the day

Tennessee's receivers accounted for the difference. Justin Jefferson scored 31.2 (projected 17.1), Amon-Ra St. Brown 28.7 and Nico Collins 21.2. Tennessee got the win despite a wrong call at tight end: Isaiah Likely scored 27.8 on the bench while starter Harold Fannin Jr. managed 4.1.

Washington got 24.7 from Jalen Hurts, 24.2 from Javonte Williams (well above his 15.3 projection) and 14.8 from Travis Etienne Jr. But Davante Adams scored 5.6 against a 13.8 projection. Their best possible lineup would have scored 152.5, enough to win, and they left 20.8 points on the bench. The QB call wasn't the issue: Brock Purdy's 21.1 on the bench was below Hurts's 24.7.

## The Bagel Busters 142.3, Cleveland Browns 129.7: Caleb Williams has the day of the week

Caleb Williams's 37.3, against a 16.4 projection, was the best individual score of Week 1. Derrick Henry added 35.3 and Breece Hall 19.8, which covered for Terry McLaurin's 3.4 (projected 12.2). The Bagel Busters' score ranked 4th in the league.

Cleveland had the week's biggest surprise performance in Christian Watson, who scored 32.7 on a 10.9 projection, and Chris Olave added 28.2. But Saquon Barkley scored 9.0 against a 17.7 projection, and Chuba Hubbard scored 23.7 on the bench. Cleveland's best possible lineup would have scored 162.2 and won comfortably. Instead they left 32.5 points on the bench and finished 9th of 10.

## poverty 130.0, Barcelona Backshotters 111.3: poverty wins a low-scoring game

Neither team had a great week. poverty's 130.0 ranked 8th and Barcelona's 111.3 was the lowest in the league. Jonathan Taylor (25.1), DJ Moore (21.0, projected 11.6) and Bucky Irving (20.3) did enough for poverty, despite a 0.0 from Kyle Pitts Sr. and Patrick Mahomes's 21.7 sitting on the bench.

Barcelona got a big game from Ashton Jeanty (32.7, projected 17.9), plus 14.5 from Rhamondre Stevenson and 14.0 from the Jaguars D/ST. Colston Loveland scored 0.0 against a 12.0 projection, and Parker Washington scored 19.3 on the bench. Barcelona's best possible lineup would have scored 145.0, enough to win this matchup, but 33.7 points stayed on the bench.

## GDID 142.9, THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING 130.5: a 33.8-point bench score

GDID's 142.9 ranked 3rd in the league, driven by Bijan Robinson (31.3, projected 18.9), Trevor Lawrence (26.1) and Ladd McConkey (19.2). A.J. Brown scored 5.6 against a 14.2 projection, and Dalton Kincaid's 18.0 sat on the bench, but it didn't matter.

THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING got 26.6 from Jaxson Dart, 26.2 from Jaxon Smith-Njigba and 19.0 from the Steelers D/ST, while Drake London scored 5.5 on a 13.8 projection. The story is the bench: Jalen Coker scored 33.8 there, the highest bench score of any player this week. Their best possible lineup, 175.2, would have been the top score in the league. Instead, 44.7 points stayed on the bench and they lost by 12.4.
