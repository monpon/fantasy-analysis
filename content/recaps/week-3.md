# Week 3: Monday night decides it, and the league splits in half

Week 3 ended with five teams at 2-1 and five at 1-2. The league average was 135.1. The Washington football team had the high score (166.2) and The Bagel Busters the low (102.2). Jahmyr Gibbs's 41.4 was the best individual score of the week. The closest game was decided on Monday night.

*Written right after Monday Night Football; ESPN later made these scores official with no changes.*

## Burden's Monday night gives master(rage)baiters their first win

*Down 12.2 with one player left, master(rage)baiters needed 12.3 from Luther Burden III. He scored 19.8.*

@matchup 6 5

After two losses to top-two scores, master(rage)baiters finally got a close one to go their way. Going into Monday night they trailed Cleveland Browns 154.2 to 142.0, with Luther Burden III the only player left to play. He was projected for 11.2 and scored 19.8, and master(rage)baiters won 161.8 to 154.2.

Jahmyr Gibbs set it up with 41.4 points, the best of any player this week, on a 25.3 projection. George Kittle added 26.2 and Harrison Mevis 16.0. The win came despite a quiet day from Josh Allen (17.5 on a 23.6 projection), plus 3.7 from Tetairoa McMillan and 5.6 from Omarion Hampton. Bo Nix (24.1) and Jordan Addison (20.0) both scored on the bench.

Cleveland will look back at its bench. Juwan Johnson scored 26.3 there and Michael Wilson 25.9, while De'Von Achane started and scored 1.7 on a 19.4 projection. Achane is now on IR. Garrett Wilson (26.7), Tyler Shough (23.8 in his first start), Christian Watson (22.6) and Chris Olave (19.7) did their part. Cleveland's best possible lineup would have scored 185.3, more than anyone in the league scored this week.

**Next up:** master(rage)baiters face poverty; Cleveland Browns face GDID.

## Washington posts the week's top score and hands poverty its first loss

*Six Washington starters scored 18 or more in a 166.2 to 107.2 rout of one of the league's two unbeaten teams.*

@matchup 10 13

The Washington football team scored 166.2, the most in the league this week, and won by 58.9. Brock Purdy led with 31.3 on a 20.8 projection. Five others topped 18: Jeremiyah Love (21.9 on a 12.8 projection), Christian McCaffrey (21.6), Matthew Golden (21.0 in his first start), Davante Adams (20.7) and Javonte Williams (18.3). The one miss was at tight end, where Sam LaPorta scored 7.4 while Kenyon Sadiq scored 23.5 on the bench.

poverty came in 2-0 but never threatened. CeeDee Lamb scored 20.2, but Jonathan Taylor managed 9.2 on a 20.6 projection and Bucky Irving 7.8 on 15.8. The quarterback choice went wrong again: Patrick Mahomes started and scored 15.9, while Joe Burrow scored 22.6 on the bench. Jakobi Meyers added 20.4 on the bench. Even poverty's best possible lineup, 127.4, would have lost.

Both teams are now 2-1.

**Next up:** Washington face THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING; poverty face master(rage)baiters.

## Fraudulent Failures' balanced lineup beats Bijan Robinson and GDID

*Seven starters scored at least 10 points for Fraudulent Failures, and GDID's 2-0 start ends.*

@matchup 1 4

Fraudulent Failures won 136.8 to 119.9 without a single standout. Ja'Marr Chase led with 24.8, followed by Kenneth Walker III (21.3), Lamar Jackson (20.4), Trey McBride (16.5), Zay Flowers (15.4), Mike Evans (12.4) and D'Andre Swift (10.8 on Monday night). The bench was more manageable this week, with 13.5 points left there, though Wan'Dale Robinson scored 18.7 on it, his best game of the season and, like all his points so far, on the bench.

GDID's Bijan Robinson did everything he could, scoring 35.3 on a 19.5 projection. Trevor Lawrence added 19.8 and Josh Downs 12.7 in his first start of the season. But Dalton Kincaid scored 3.8 on an 11.6 projection, Chase Brown 8.9 on 16.6, and the Bengals D/ST scored 0.0. Bhayshul Tuten scored 17.0 on the bench. GDID's best possible lineup, 133.1, would still have lost.

Both teams are 2-1.

**Next up:** Fraudulent Failures face Barcelona Backshotters; GDID face Cleveland Browns.

## Smith-Njigba, London and Bowers bury Tennessee

*THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING scored 162.9, and Tennessee's scoring dropped for a third straight week.*

@matchup 9 7

THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING won by 56.2, 162.9 to 106.8. Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 35.4, his third straight week over 26, and Drake London added 29.4. Brock Bowers scored 27.6 in his first start of the season, after 0.0 on the bench in each of the first two weeks. Jared Goff scored 19.4 at quarterback with Jaxson Dart on IR.

One call didn't work: Jalen Coker got his first start after scoring 48.4 on the bench over the first two weeks, and scored 3.8 on a 13.8 projection.

Tennessee's receivers, their biggest strength, went quiet. Amon-Ra St. Brown scored 11.9 on a 19.9 projection and Justin Jefferson 5.2 on 18.3. Drake Maye scored 4.8 on an 18.6 projection, Jadarian Price 1.2, and Isaiah Likely 3.3 at tight end while Harold Fannin Jr. scored 24.1 on the bench. Kyren Williams (21.8), Tee Higgins (21.0) and Jaylen Warren (20.6) kept it from being worse. Tennessee has now scored 142.2, 114.9 and 106.8.

**Next up:** THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING face The Washington football team; Tennessee Top G's face The Bagel Busters.

## New arrivals help Barcelona to its first win

*Sam Darnold scored 27.7 in his first start for Barcelona, and The Bagel Busters had the week's lowest score.*

@matchup 8 11

Barcelona Backshotters won their first game, 132.7 to 102.2, with help from a recent pickup. Sam Darnold scored 27.7 at quarterback on a 15.8 projection. James Cook III added 19.4, the Vikings D/ST scored 19.0 on a 6.5 projection, and George Pickens 15.2. Saquon Barkley scored 9.0 on a 17.4 projection in his first game for Barcelona. Colston Loveland scored 7.1, more than his first two weeks combined (1.3). Barcelona left only 4.3 points on the bench, the smallest gap of any team this week.

The Bagel Busters' 102.2 was the lowest score in the league. Derrick Henry (21.9), Terry McLaurin (19.7) and Dak Prescott (18.9) led the way, but Oronde Gadsden scored 0.0 at FLEX and the 49ers D/ST 0.0 as well. Matthew Stafford (20.9), Keenan Allen (18.3) and Malik Washington (11.1) all scored on the bench. Even their best possible lineup, 127.6, wouldn't have been enough.

**Next up:** Barcelona Backshotters face Fraudulent Failures; The Bagel Busters face Tennessee Top G's.
