# Week 5: the favorites fall, and Net Worth: 1.4b takes sole possession of first

Week 5 was the highest-scoring week of the season, with a league average of 142.5. It was also bad for favorites: four of the five teams ESPN projected to win as of Thursday night lost. Tennessee Top G's had the high score (195.8) for a second straight week, and GDID's 90.5 was the lowest score of the season. Drake Maye's 39.5 was the best individual game.

Another team has a new name: master(rage)baiters are now **gibbs and friends**. After five weeks, Net Worth: 1.4b is alone in first at 4-1. Three teams are 3-2, and six are 2-3.

## Net Worth: 1.4b wins the battle for first

*Goff, Smith-Njigba and Bowers lead the way, and poverty's former kicker scores 16.0 against them.*

@matchup 9 13

In a matchup of 3-1 teams, Net Worth: 1.4b beat poverty 146.0 to 137.9 and moved into sole possession of first place. Jared Goff scored 28.9 on a 19.4 projection, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 23.8, Brock Bowers 23.0, Drake London 19.8 and Aaron Jones Sr. 18.7. Will Reichard, who kicked for poverty in Weeks 1 and 2, scored 16.0. Deebo Samuel scored 2.5 and is now listed out.

poverty got 31.5 from Bucky Irving, more than double his 15.2 projection, and 24.6 from Joe Burrow, starting with Patrick Mahomes on bye. Spencer Shrader kicked for 21.0. But CeeDee Lamb scored 2.9 on a 19.1 projection, and Jonathan Taylor 11.2 on 19.0. Blake Corum (16.2), the Seahawks D/ST (11.0) and Khalil Shakir (11.0) scored on the bench, and poverty's best possible lineup, 157.6, would have won.

**Next up:** Net Worth: 1.4b face gibbs and friends; poverty face The Bagel Busters.

## Maye's 39.5 leads Tennessee to a second straight huge week

*Fraudulent Failures scored 159.9, the 3rd-highest score of the week, and lost by 35.9.*

@matchup 7 1

Tennessee Top G's followed their 207.1 with 195.8. Drake Maye, who averaged 7.5 points over his first three starts, scored 39.5 on an 18.5 projection, the best individual score of the week. Jaylen Warren added 36.8, and Isaiah Likely scored 32.4 at tight end. That was the first week the starting tight end outscored the benched one (Harold Fannin Jr., 1.4). Kyren Williams (19.6) and Justin Jefferson (18.8) contributed too. Amon-Ra St. Brown scored 12.9 on a 20.0 projection.

Fraudulent Failures did almost everything right. With Kenneth Walker III on bye and Lamar Jackson projected for 0, Jacoby Brissett started and scored 25.3. Mike Evans added 26.3 on a 12.8 projection, Ja'Marr Chase 22.7, Trey McBride 21.6 and D'Andre Swift 17.3. They left just 6.0 points on the bench. Even their best possible lineup, 165.9, would have lost.

**Next up:** Tennessee Top G's face Cleveland Browns; Fraudulent Failures face The Washington football team.

## Barcelona scores 181.0, its best week by almost 50 points

*Jayden Daniels, Malik Nabers and George Pickens all topped 28, and Cleveland's 32.3-point receiver sat on the bench.*

@matchup 8 5

Barcelona Backshotters scored 181.0, the 2nd-highest score of the week and their best of the season by 48.3 points. Jayden Daniels scored 32.3 on a 20.5 projection. Malik Nabers (29.1) and George Pickens (28.0) both more than doubled their projections. Jason Myers kicked for 23.0, James Cook III added 22.8, and Rhamondre Stevenson 17.5. They left only 5.0 points on the bench.

Cleveland Browns scored 135.0, which would have beaten three teams. Parker Washington, who started the season on Barcelona's bench, scored 30.4 against his former team. Garrett Wilson added 23.4, and Kyler Murray, newly arrived at quarterback, scored 18.3. But Christian Watson scored 32.3 on the bench, the highest bench score of any player this week, while Chris Olave (9.6) and Michael Wilson (8.3) started. Even Cleveland's best possible lineup (163.0) would have lost.

**Next up:** Barcelona Backshotters face GDID; Cleveland Browns face Tennessee Top G's.

## Nacua's 37.7 lifts the Bagel Busters past Washington

*Washington had 150.5 points available on its roster and scored 118.7.*

@matchup 11 10

The Bagel Busters won 135.8 to 118.7, led by Puka Nacua with 37.7 on a 21.7 projection. Derrick Henry added 21.0, Jameson Williams 18.7 and Brenton Strange 13.8. They won despite another wrong quarterback call: Dak Prescott started and scored 12.6, while Matthew Stafford scored 27.3 on the bench.

Washington got 22.1 from Davante Adams, 20.3 from Matthew Golden, 19.8 from Sam LaPorta and 17.3 from Christian McCaffrey. The damage came from Jeremiyah Love (2.2 on a 14.4 projection), Carnell Tate (1.6 on 13.5) and the Colts D/ST (-1.0). Meanwhile Tyler Warren scored 21.4 on the bench, Jacory Croskey-Merritt 14.2 and Denzel Boston 13.6. Washington's best possible lineup would have scored 150.5 and won. It's Washington's second straight loss in which a better lineup was available.

**Next up:** The Bagel Busters face poverty; The Washington football team face Fraudulent Failures.

## gibbs and friends beat GDID's season-low 90.5

*Jahmyr Gibbs scored 37.7 for the newly renamed team, and GDID has now lost three straight.*

@matchup 6 4

gibbs and friends won 124.3 to 90.5. The team's namesake, Jahmyr Gibbs, scored 37.7 on a 27.0 projection, and Josh Allen added 30.5. The Broncos D/ST scored 14.0, and Woody Marks 12.5 in his first start. David Montgomery scored 1.1 and is now listed out. Their 124.3 ranked 8th this week. GDID's Trevor Lawrence (21.7) and Ka'imi Fairbairn (17.0) were the top scorers. Bijan Robinson scored 15.8 on a 22.2 projection, Chase Brown 7.0 on 17.4, Roman Wilson 0.0 and the Bears D/ST -2.0. Even GDID's best possible lineup (99.8) would have lost. After starting 2-0, GDID is 2-3 and last in the standings.

**Next up:** gibbs and friends face Net Worth: 1.4b; GDID face Barcelona Backshotters.
