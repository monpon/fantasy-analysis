# Week 2 recaps: poverty goes off, and the bench bites back

Scoring dropped to a league average of 128.1. poverty set the season high so far with 192.5, and The Bagel Busters had the low of 91.2. The week had both the closest game of the season (2.4 points) and the biggest blowout (77.6). The top starter was Jaxon Smith-Njigba with 42.5.

For four of the five losing teams, the best possible lineup from their own roster would have won the game.

## The Washington football team 153.0, master(rage)baiters 150.6: the closest game of the season

The week's 2nd- and 3rd-highest scores met, and only 2.4 points separated them. Davante Adams won it for Washington with 40.5 points against a 15.5 projection, a turnaround from his 5.6 in Week 1. Brock Purdy added 28.5 in his first start and Christian McCaffrey 22.6. Javonte Williams scored 8.0 on a 16.7 projection, and Matthew Stafford's 27.0 sat on the bench.

master(rage)baiters were close to winning again. Josh Allen scored 40.8, well above his 22.7 projection, Jahmyr Gibbs added 23.3 and George Kittle 18.0. David Montgomery's 4.4 (projected 14.2) hurt, and Terrance Ferguson scored 17.4 on the bench. Their best possible lineup, 165.9, would have won. Their 150.6 would have beaten seven of the nine teams, but they're 0-2 after losing to the week's top scorer in Week 1 and the 2nd-highest score in Week 2.

## Cleveland Browns 139.6, Fraudulent Failures 124.6: the Week 1 high scorers come back to earth

Fraudulent Failures fell from 174.3 to 124.6. Ja'Marr Chase recovered with 26.5, Kenneth Walker III scored 23.8 and Trey McBride 18.1, but Dallas Goedert scored 1.4 against a 10.8 projection. That came one week after he scored 23.7 on their bench. Rashod Bateman scored 22.8 on the bench this time. Their best possible lineup would have scored 151.7 and won. They left 27.1 points on the bench.

Cleveland got 24.1 from Bryce Young (projected 16.6), 22.6 from Chris Olave and 17.0 from Brandon Aubrey. Saquon Barkley scored 3.0 on a 17.6 projection, and Tyler Shough scored 22.4 on the bench. Cleveland ranked 4th this week and evened its record at 1-1.

## poverty 192.5, Tennessee Top G's 114.9: the biggest win of the season

poverty's 192.5 was the highest score of the season and beat Tennessee by 77.6. CeeDee Lamb scored 35.3 (projected 18.0), Jonathan Taylor 29.2 and Patrick Mahomes 29.0. They did it even with DJ Moore scoring -0.1 against a 12.6 projection. They left only 10.3 points on the bench.

Tennessee's Amon-Ra St. Brown scored 35.2, well above his 20.4 projection, with Kyren Williams at 15.7 and Tee Higgins at 14.5. Drake Maye scored 8.0 against an 18.2 projection. It didn't matter much: Tennessee's best possible lineup (127.8) would still have lost by more than 60.

## GDID 99.2, The Bagel Busters 91.2: the week's lowest-scoring game

This was the week's lowest-scoring game: GDID's 99.2 ranked 8th and The Bagel Busters' 91.2 ranked 10th. GDID won it with Dalton Kincaid (22.5, more than double his 10.4 projection), Bhayshul Tuten (14.2) and Chase Brown (11.2). Bijan Robinson scored 11.1 against a 22.9 projection, and Travis Kelce's 25.1 sat on the bench. GDID is 2-0.

The Bagel Busters will regret this one. They started Caleb Williams, who scored 7.7 against a 19.4 projection, while Dak Prescott scored 29.8 on the bench. Their best possible lineup would have scored 135.4 and won easily. They left 44.1 points on the bench, more than any other team this week. Jake Ferguson (20.3), Derrick Henry (17.7) and Breece Hall (14.2) were their top starters.

## THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING 123.1, Barcelona Backshotters 92.7: Smith-Njigba's big day

Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 42.5, more than double his 18.5 projection, for the best individual score of the week. Harrison Butker added 17.0 and Drake London 10.9. Jaxson Dart scored 0.8 against an 18.2 projection. THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING is 1-1.

Barcelona got 21.8 from Jaylen Waddle, 20.9 from James Cook III and 14.7 from Jayden Daniels. Malik Nabers scored 1.1 against a 14.9 projection, and Parker Washington scored 17.8 on the bench for a second straight week. Barcelona's best possible lineup would have scored 128.9 and won. Instead they left 36.2 points on the bench and fell to 0-2.
