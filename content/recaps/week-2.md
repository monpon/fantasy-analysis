# Week 2: poverty's 192.5, a 2.4-point thriller, and a lot of benched points

The league average fell to 128.1. poverty set the season high with 192.5, and The Bagel Busters had the low of 91.2. The week had both the closest game so far (2.4 points) and the biggest blowout (77.6). For four of the five losing teams, the best possible lineup from their own roster would have won.

## Adams's 40.5 lifts Washington past master(rage)baiters by 2.4

*The week's second- and third-highest scores met, and master(rage)baiters fell to 0-2 despite a 40.8 from Josh Allen.*

@matchup 10 6

This was the game of the week. The Washington football team won 153.0 to 150.6, with both teams scoring more than all but one other team in the league. The difference came from a receiver who had disappointed a week earlier: Davante Adams scored 40.5 on a 15.5 projection, after scoring 5.6 in Week 1.

Brock Purdy, in his first start of the season, scored 28.5. Christian McCaffrey added 22.6 and Sam LaPorta 17.2. It was enough even though the running backs struggled: Javonte Williams (8.0), Jeremiyah Love (7.5) and Travis Etienne Jr. (7.1) all came in under 13 points. Matthew Stafford scored 27.0 on the bench and Denzel Boston 20.5.

master(rage)baiters had every chance. Josh Allen scored 40.8, well above his 22.7 projection, Jahmyr Gibbs added 23.3 and George Kittle 18.0. The damage came from David Montgomery (4.4 on 14.2), Luther Burden III (6.2 on 12.5) and Harrison Mevis (4.0 on 9.4), with Terrance Ferguson scoring 17.4 on the bench. Their best possible lineup, 165.9, would have won. After losing to the week's top scorer in Week 1 and its second-highest scorer in Week 2, they're 0-2.

**Next up:** Washington face poverty; master(rage)baiters face Cleveland Browns.

## Bryce Young and a balanced lineup lift Cleveland past Fraudulent Failures

*Fraudulent Failures dropped from 174.3 to 124.6, and Cleveland evened its record at 1-1.*

@matchup 5 1

Cleveland Browns won 139.6 to 124.6 with a balanced performance. Bryce Young scored 24.1 on a 16.6 projection, Chris Olave 22.6, Brandon Aubrey 17.0 and Garrett Wilson 16.7. The shortfalls were at running back and defense: Saquon Barkley scored 3.0 on a 17.6 projection, De'Von Achane 12.3 on 17.1, and the Chiefs D/ST 1.0.

Fraudulent Failures got 26.5 from Ja'Marr Chase and 23.8 from Kenneth Walker III, but the rest came in short. Lamar Jackson scored 14.8 on a 21.7 projection, and Dallas Goedert scored 1.4 on 10.8, one week after scoring 23.7 on their bench. This time Rashod Bateman scored 22.8 on the bench.

Both teams left points behind. Cleveland's Tyler Shough scored 22.4 and Stefon Diggs 21.7 on the bench. Fraudulent Failures' best possible lineup would have scored 151.7 and won.

**Next up:** Cleveland Browns face master(rage)baiters; Fraudulent Failures face GDID.

## poverty's 192.5 buries Tennessee by 77.6

*Five starters scored 26 or more in the season's highest score, even with a -0.1 from DJ Moore.*

@matchup 13 7

poverty scored 192.5, the most by any team this season, and it was never close. CeeDee Lamb scored 35.3, Jonathan Taylor 29.2, Patrick Mahomes 29.0 and DeVonta Smith 28.7, and Dalton Schultz added 26.0 on an 8.9 projection. They did it even with DJ Moore scoring -0.1 on a 12.6 projection, and they left only 10.3 points on the bench.

Tennessee's Amon-Ra St. Brown was the lone standout with 35.2, well over his 20.4 projection. Justin Jefferson scored 8.5 on 18.4, Drake Maye 8.0 on 18.2, and Jadarian Price 5.0 on 13.2. Even Tennessee's best possible lineup, 127.8, would have lost by more than 60.

**Next up:** poverty face The Washington football team; Tennessee Top G's face THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING.

## GDID wins the week's lowest-scoring game to move to 2-0

*GDID's 99.2 was enough, because The Bagel Busters left Dak Prescott's 29.8 on the bench.*

@matchup 4 11

The two teams combined for 190.4 points, the lowest total of any game this week. GDID's 99.2 ranked 8th in the league, and The Bagel Busters' 91.2 ranked last. Dalton Kincaid won it for GDID with 22.5 on a 10.4 projection, while Bhayshul Tuten added 14.2. Bijan Robinson scored 11.1 on a 22.9 projection and Trevor Lawrence 6.2 on 15.9. Travis Kelce scored 25.1 on the bench.

The Bagel Busters' decisions cost them the game. They started Caleb Williams (7.7 on a 19.4 projection) while Dak Prescott scored 29.8 on the bench, and Tre Tucker scored 22.9 there too. Their best possible lineup would have scored 135.4. They left 44.1 points on the bench, more than any other team this week. Jake Ferguson (20.3) and Derrick Henry (17.7) led their starters.

**Next up:** GDID face Fraudulent Failures; The Bagel Busters face Barcelona Backshotters.

## Smith-Njigba's 42.5 carries THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING past Barcelona

*The best individual score of the week decided a game that Barcelona could have won with its bench.*

@matchup 9 8

Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 42.5, more than double his 18.5 projection, and THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING won 123.1 to 92.7. Harrison Butker added 17.0, but no other starter topped 11 points. Jaxson Dart scored 0.8 on an 18.2 projection.

Barcelona fell to 0-2. Jaylen Waddle scored 21.8 and James Cook III 20.9, but Malik Nabers scored 1.1 on a 14.9 projection, Rhamondre Stevenson 3.6, and Colston Loveland 1.3. That left Loveland with 1.3 points in two starts. Parker Washington scored 17.8 on the bench, his second straight big game there, and Xavier Worthy scored 13.5. Barcelona's best possible lineup would have scored 128.9 and won.

**Next up:** THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING face Tennessee Top G's; Barcelona Backshotters face The Bagel Busters.
