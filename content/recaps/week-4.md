# Week 4: Tennessee drops 207.1, and three teams reach 3-1

Tennessee Top G's posted the highest score of the season, 207.1, and won by 84.4, also a season record. GDID had the week's low score (110.8), and the league average was 137.1. Tetairoa McMillan scored 45.2, the best individual performance of the week, and his team still lost. It was also a busy week for roster moves across the league. One team has a new name: THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING is now **Net Worth: 1.4b**.

After four weeks, poverty, Fraudulent Failures and Net Worth: 1.4b are 3-1. Four teams are 2-2, and three are 1-3.

## Lamb's 41.3 carries poverty past master(rage)baiters

*McMillan scored 45.2 for master(rage)baiters, and they still fell to 1-3.*

@matchup 13 6

poverty won 149.0 to 134.4 with a big game from CeeDee Lamb: 41.3 points on a 16.9 projection. Jonathan Taylor added 22.7, new tight end Tucker Kraft 17.5 on an 8.9 projection, and Devaughn Vele 17.4. DJ Moore scored 2.7 and Bucky Irving 6.1.

The quarterback decision went wrong for the third time in four weeks. Patrick Mahomes started and scored 17.0, and Joe Burrow scored 23.7 on the bench. It didn't cost them this time.

master(rage)baiters got the best individual score of the week from Tetairoa McMillan, 45.2 on a 15.8 projection. But almost everything else came in under. Jahmyr Gibbs scored 17.7 on a 26.1 projection, Josh Allen 18.5, David Montgomery 4.3, and Rashee Rice 0.0 on 14.9. Their best possible lineup, 153.1, would have won. master(rage)baiters still lead the league in points per game and all-play, and are 1-3.

**Next up:** poverty face Net Worth: 1.4b; master(rage)baiters face GDID.

## Cleveland's new tight end helps beat GDID

*T.J. Hockenson scored 24.9 on a 9.0 projection, and GDID had the week's lowest score.*

@matchup 5 4

Cleveland Browns won 135.9 to 110.8. Chuba Hubbard led with 25.9, and T.J. Hockenson, recently added from Washington, scored 24.9 on a 9.0 projection. Chris Olave added 19.6 and Michael Wilson 16.5. Garrett Wilson scored 5.7 on a 16.5 projection. The bench had its usual output: Alvin Kamara scored 22.8 there and Bryce Young 21.5, while Tyler Shough started at QB and scored 15.9.

GDID's 110.8 was the lowest score in the league. Bijan Robinson scored 27.7 and Chase Brown 20.1, but the receivers scored next to nothing again: Ladd McConkey 0.0 on an 11.7 projection, Josh Downs 4.6 and Makai Lemon 5.7. Travis Kelce scored 3.5 at tight end. Quinshon Judkins scored 21.6 on the bench. GDID's best possible lineup, 143.2, would have won.

Both teams are 2-2.

**Next up:** Cleveland Browns face Barcelona Backshotters; GDID face master(rage)baiters.

## Net Worth: 1.4b edges Washington by 3.3 in the closest game of the week

*Washington left 44.5 points on its bench, including both of the quarterbacks it didn't start.*

@matchup 9 10

The renamed Net Worth: 1.4b won 125.0 to 121.7 with a balanced lineup. Brock Bowers scored 20.6, Jared Goff 20.5, Deebo Samuel 18.0 in his first start (projected 10.8) and Aaron Jones Sr. 16.8. That was enough even though Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 12.6 on a 21.9 projection, his first week below 26 points this season.

Washington had the points to win on its roster. Javonte Williams scored 31.3 on a 14.6 projection and Sam LaPorta 22.4. But Washington started newly added Kyler Murray at quarterback (11.5), while Brock Purdy scored 19.6 and Jalen Hurts 13.5 on the bench. Romeo Doubs (23.8) and Carnell Tate (21.5) also scored on the bench, while starters Davante Adams (7.2) and Jeremiyah Love (6.5) came in well under projection. Washington's best possible lineup would have scored 166.2 and won comfortably. They left 44.5 points on the bench, second-most in the league this week.

**Next up:** Net Worth: 1.4b face poverty; The Washington football team face The Bagel Busters.

## Tennessee's 207.1 is the highest score of the season

*Five Tennessee starters topped 23 points, and the Bagel Busters' near-perfect lineup didn't come close.*

@matchup 7 11

After three straight weeks of falling scores, Tennessee Top G's scored 207.1 and won by 84.4, the highest score and biggest margin of the season. Kyren Williams scored 37.7 on a 13.1 projection. Nico Collins added 30.8 in his first start since Week 1, new addition Emanuel Wilson 28.0 on an 11.5 projection, Tee Higgins 26.7, and C.J. Stroud 23.1 in his first start at quarterback. Tennessee left only 5.0 points on the bench, with Drake Maye scoring 26.2 there.

The Bagel Busters did almost everything right and lost by more than 80. Their lineup was within 1.2 points of the best possible, the smallest gap of any team this week. Puka Nacua scored 27.7 in his return, Dak Prescott 18.1 and Jameson Williams 16.2. Derrick Henry scored 15.9 on a 20.3 projection. Their 122.7 would have beaten two teams this week.

**Next up:** Tennessee Top G's face Fraudulent Failures; The Bagel Busters face The Washington football team.
