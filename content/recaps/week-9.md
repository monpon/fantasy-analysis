# Week 9: Washington snaps its skid with the week's top score, and Tennessee wins a sixth straight

The Washington football team scored 166.5, the week's high and its best of the season, to end a five-game losing streak. Tennessee Top G's won a sixth straight and now has first place to itself at 7-2. JSN merchant had the week's low score (99.0) and fell to 6-3, level with Fraudulent Failures. Kenneth Walker III's 36.3 was the best game by a starter. The league average was 130.6.

It was also a week of quarterback decisions that went wrong: Jordan Love (36.2), C.J. Stroud (35.4) and Bryce Young (20.3) all scored more on the bench than their team's starting quarterback.

## Washington ends its losing streak with a 62.3-point rout

*Matthew Golden scored 32.4, and Washington's 166.5 was its best score of the season.*

@matchup 10 8

The Washington football team beat Barcelona Backshotters 166.5 to 104.2, the biggest margin of the week. With Brock Purdy and Christian McCaffrey back from their bye, most of the lineup delivered. Matthew Golden scored 32.4 on a 17.3 projection, Purdy 25.3, McCaffrey 22.7 and Javonte Williams 22.4. The 49ers D/ST added 19.0 on a 5.6 projection. Michael Wilson, on Cleveland's roster a week earlier, scored 12.0 in his first start for Washington. The tight ends were the weak spot again: Tyler Warren scored 8.4 and Sam LaPorta 7.2.

Barcelona's quarterback choice went the wrong way for a second straight week. Jayden Daniels started and scored 14.6 on a 19.8 projection, while Jordan Love scored 36.2 on the bench. Tank Dell added 20.5 on the bench, and Barcelona left 44.3 points there, the most of any team this week. James Cook III led the starters with 16.7. Malik Nabers scored 9.3, Ashton Jeanty 7.0 and Jaylen Waddle 3.2. Even Barcelona's best possible lineup (148.5) would have lost.

**Next up:** Washington faces Tennessee Top G's; Barcelona faces i hate darius cooper.

## Tennessee wins a sixth straight and takes sole possession of first

*Tee Higgins led a balanced lineup, and friends' stars came in well under their projections.*

@matchup 7 6

Tennessee Top G's beat friends 133.3 to 115.5. No Tennessee starter scored more than 20.2, but most were solid: Tee Higgins scored 20.2, Drake Maye 18.3, Amon-Ra St. Brown 17.6 and Nico Collins 15.5. The Chiefs D/ST (12.0 on 6.0) and Evan McPherson (13.0 on 7.5) both beat their projections. Justin Jefferson scored 8.8 on an 18.3 projection, Kyren Williams 9.1 and Tyler Allgeier 6.6. Isaiah Likely scored 16.3 on the bench, ahead of starting tight end Harold Fannin Jr. (12.2).

friends got 21.7 from Luther Burden III on a 13.9 projection and 15.6 from Tetairoa McMillan, but their two best players came in low. Jahmyr Gibbs scored 13.4 on a 26.5 projection and Josh Allen 12.9 on 19.9. George Kittle added 9.8. friends' lineup was within 2.5 points of its best, and even that (118.0) wouldn't have been enough. It's their third straight loss.

**Next up:** Tennessee faces Washington; friends face Fraudulent Failures.

## Fraudulent Failures beat the Bagel Busters with Walker and Chase

*Kenneth Walker III scored 36.3 and Ja'Marr Chase 31.5, while C.J. Stroud scored 35.4 on the bench.*

@matchup 1 11

**Next up:** Fraudulent Failures face friends; the Bagel Busters face Cleveland Browns.

@matchup 5 9
