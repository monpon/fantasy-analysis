# Week 4 preview: five first-time matchups, and two lineups with holes

Every Week 4 matchup is a first meeting. ESPN's projections favor master(rage)baiters, Cleveland Browns, THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING, Fraudulent Failures and Tennessee Top G's. Two of those edges, 25.0 and 35.7 points, come partly from the underdog starting a player projected for zero points.

*Projections are ESPN's as of Tuesday, Sept. 29, based on the lineups set at that time. Lineups and projections will change before kickoff.*

## The league's best all-play team gets a chance to climb against poverty

*master(rage)baiters are 1-2 despite leading the league in scoring. poverty is 2-1 despite a below-.500 all-play record.*

@matchup 6 13

On paper, the teams' records are the wrong way round. master(rage)baiters lead the league in points per game (152.0) and all-play (22-5) but are 1-2. poverty is 2-1 with a 13-14 all-play record. ESPN projects a close game: master(rage)baiters 139.7, poverty 136.6.

**What to watch:** Jahmyr Gibbs's projection of 26.1 is the highest in the matchup. Josh Allen is listed questionable (projected 22.3), with Bo Nix (15.3) available on the bench. For poverty, the quarterback decision has gone wrong in two of three weeks. Patrick Mahomes (18.6) is currently starting over Joe Burrow (18.2), a projection gap of 0.4. Jonathan Taylor (20.4) and CeeDee Lamb (16.9) are poverty's top projected players.

## GDID's running backs meet Cleveland's receivers

*The league's 3rd-ranked backfield and last-ranked receivers, against the top-ranked receivers and 9th-ranked backfield.*

@matchup 4 5

These teams are built in opposite ways. GDID ranks 3rd at running back and last at receiver. Cleveland ranks 1st at receiver and 9th at running back. GDID is 2-1 with 120.6 points per game, and Cleveland is 1-2 with 141.2. ESPN projects Cleveland 137.1, GDID 131.0.

**What to watch:** Bijan Robinson is projected for 21.5, and GDID's starting lineup includes both Travis Kelce and Dalton Kincaid. For Cleveland, Tyler Shough is starting (projected 18.9) with Bryce Young on the bench, projected slightly higher at 20.1. Juwan Johnson has 51.3 bench points this season and is on the bench again (projected 10.6). Cleveland's receivers, Chris Olave (18.3), Garrett Wilson (15.5), Parker Washington (15.3) and Christian Watson (14.4), are projected to outscore GDID's.

## First place Washington visits a THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING team coming off a 162.9

*ESPN has THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING by 25.0, and part of that gap is an injured player in Washington's lineup.*

@matchup 9 10

Washington is in 1st place and ranks 2nd in points per game (150.3). THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING is 2-1 after a 162.9 in Week 3. ESPN's projections as of Tuesday: THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING 146.5, Washington 121.4.

That 25-point gap is misleading for now. Washington's current lineup includes Travis Etienne Jr., who is listed out and projected for 0.0. With its best available lineup, Washington projects to 133.3, which still leaves THAT'S MY FAVORITE SAYING ahead by 13.2.

**What to watch:** Jaxon Smith-Njigba, the league's top scorer, is projected for 21.0, and Brock Bowers for 15.9 after his 27.6 last week. Jalen Coker is starting while listed questionable. For Washington, Christian McCaffrey (20.2) and Brock Purdy (18.6) lead the projections, and Jalen Hurts (18.1), Carnell Tate (11.9) and Alvin Kamara (10.6) are on the bench.

## Barcelona's new lineup gets a test against Fraudulent Failures

*Barcelona is coming off its best week. Fraudulent Failures are 2-1 and favored by 6.3.*

@matchup 8 1

Barcelona won its first game in Week 3, 132.7 to 102.2, after adding Sam Darnold and Saquon Barkley. Fraudulent Failures are 2nd in the standings and 3rd in all-play (19-8). ESPN projects Fraudulent Failures 141.9, Barcelona 135.6. That's the week's third-closest projection, and Barcelona's current lineup already matches its best projected lineup.

**What to watch:** Barcelona's backfield of Ashton Jeanty (18.8), James Cook III (17.5) and Barkley (15.7) against Fraudulent Failures' top three: Lamar Jackson (21.1), Kenneth Walker III (20.5) and Ja'Marr Chase (20.5). At tight end, Colston Loveland and Mark Andrews are both projected for 10.0. Mike Evans is listed questionable and currently starting (12.4). Fraudulent Failures' Panthers D/ST has the lowest projection of any starting defense in the league this week, 2.6.

## The Bagel Busters need help before they face Tennessee

*ESPN projects Tennessee by 35.7, the biggest gap of the week, and even the Bagel Busters' best lineup is well short.*

@matchup 11 7

Both teams are 1-2 and in the bottom three of the standings. Tennessee's scoring has dropped every week (142.2, 114.9, 106.8), and the Bagel Busters had the league's lowest score in each of the last two weeks. ESPN projects Tennessee 133.8, the Bagel Busters 98.1.

The Bagel Busters' current lineup has problems. Breece Hall is listed questionable and projected for 0.0, and three tight ends are starting, including Oronde Gadsden at FLEX (7.1). Keenan Allen (11.2), Courtland Sutton (10.6) and Jameson Williams (10.4) are on the bench. Even so, their best available lineup projects to 105.3, about 28 points behind Tennessee.

**What to watch:** The top projections in this matchup are the Bagel Busters' Derrick Henry (20.3) and Tennessee's Amon-Ra St. Brown (20.1) and Justin Jefferson (18.6, listed questionable). Drake Maye, who has averaged 7.5 points per start, is projected for 17.5. At tight end, Isaiah Likely is starting (11.7) and Harold Fannin Jr. is on the bench (11.2). The benched tight end has outscored the starter every week so far.
