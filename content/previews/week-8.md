# Week 8 preview: the top two meet, and two games are projected within 1.1

The biggest game of the week is Fraudulent Failures vs. JSN merchant, two of the three 5-2 teams. Two more games, friends vs. the Bagel Busters and Tennessee vs. Barcelona, are projected within about a point. *Written Thursday night, after the Panthers–Packers game had kicked off. Projections are ESPN's pre-game numbers for the lineups set at that time, and lineups can still change for Sunday. Once games are played, each matchup box below shows the live score.*
