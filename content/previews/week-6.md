That takes Jahmyr Gibbs, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jared Goff, Ja'Marr Chase, Justin Jefferson and others out of action, and several fantasy lineups are noticeably thinner for it. ESPN's closest projection is Fraudulent Failures vs. Washington, at 2.7 points.
